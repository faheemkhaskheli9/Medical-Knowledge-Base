# Pediatrics

- Dose by **weight (mg/kg)** and never exceed the adult maximum dose.
- Always confirm the **formulation concentration** (e.g. mg per 5 mL) — a frequent source of 10× errors.
- Use age-specific vital-sign and lab reference ranges.

## Avoid in children (examples)
| Drug | Concern |
|------|---------|
| Aspirin (< 16 years, UK; children/teenagers, US) | Reye's syndrome (except specific indications, e.g. Kawasaki disease) |
| Codeine, tramadol (< 12 years; post-tonsillectomy < 18 years) | Fatal respiratory depression in ultra-rapid CYP2D6 metabolizers |
| Tetracyclines (< 8 years, US; < 12 years, UK) | Tooth discoloration (doxycycline short courses may be acceptable per guidance) |
| Fluoroquinolones | Musculoskeletal effects — reserve for specific indications |
| OTC cough/cold medicines (< 2 years US; < 6 years UK) | Toxicity without proven benefit |
| Honey (< 12 months) | Infant botulism |
