# Renal and Hepatic Impairment

## Renal
- Drug labels use **Cockcroft-Gault CrCl** (mL/min) for many dose adjustments; eGFR (CKD-EPI) is used for CKD staging. Check which the label specifies. Formulas: [../clinical-reference/formulas.md](../clinical-reference/formulas.md).
- Common drugs needing renal adjustment/avoidance: metformin, DOACs, gabapentin/pregabalin, many antibiotics (aminoglycosides, vancomycin, nitrofurantoin), digoxin, lithium, LMWH, allopurinol, sulfonylureas.
- Nephrotoxins: NSAIDs, aminoglycosides, vancomycin, IV contrast, amphotericin B, calcineurin inhibitors.

## CKD stages (KDIGO, eGFR mL/min/1.73 m²)
G1 ≥ 90 · G2 60–89 · G3a 45–59 · G3b 30–44 · G4 15–29 · G5 < 15

## Hepatic
- Child-Pugh class (A/B/C) is commonly used in labeling for dose adjustment.
- Caution: paracetamol (lower max), opioids, benzodiazepines (prefer lorazepam/oxazepam), NSAIDs (renal and bleeding risk), drugs causing encephalopathy.
