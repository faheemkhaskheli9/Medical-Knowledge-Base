# Clinical Scores

Scores support, not replace, clinical judgment.

## CHA₂DS₂-VASc — stroke risk in atrial fibrillation
| Factor | Points |
|--------|--------|
| **C**ongestive heart failure | 1 |
| **H**ypertension | 1 |
| **A**ge ≥ 75 | 2 |
| **D**iabetes | 1 |
| **S**troke / TIA / thromboembolism | 2 |
| **V**ascular disease (MI, PAD, aortic plaque) | 1 |
| **A**ge 65–74 | 1 |
| **S**ex **c**ategory female | 1 |

- ACC/AHA 2023: oral anticoagulation recommended at score ≥ 2 (men) or ≥ 3 (women); reasonable at 1 (men) / 2 (women).
- ESC 2024 uses **CHA₂DS₂-VA** (sex removed): anticoagulation recommended at ≥ 2, should be considered at 1.

## HAS-BLED — bleeding risk on anticoagulation
1 point each: **H**ypertension (uncontrolled, SBP > 160), **A**bnormal renal function, **A**bnormal liver function, **S**troke, **B**leeding history/predisposition, **L**abile INR, **E**lderly (> 65), **D**rugs (antiplatelets/NSAIDs), **D**rugs: alcohol excess. Max 9.

Score ≥ 3 = high bleeding risk → correct modifiable factors and review more often. **Not** a reason by itself to withhold anticoagulation.

## CURB-65 — community-acquired pneumonia severity
1 point each: **C**onfusion; **U**rea > 7 mmol/L (BUN > 19 mg/dL); **R**espiratory rate ≥ 30; **B**lood pressure SBP < 90 or DBP ≤ 60 mmHg; age ≥ **65**.

| Score | 30-day mortality (approx.) | Suggested setting |
|-------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| 0–1 | Low (< 3%) | Consider outpatient |
| 2 | Intermediate (~9%) | Consider hospital |
| 3–5 | High (15–40%) | Hospital; consider ICU at 4–5 |

## qSOFA
1 point each: respiratory rate ≥ 22, altered mentation, SBP ≤ 100 mmHg. Score ≥ 2 = higher risk of poor outcome. Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021 recommends **against** using qSOFA alone as a sepsis screening tool.

## Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)
| Eye (E) | Verbal (V) | Motor (M) |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| 4 Spontaneous | 5 Oriented | 6 Obeys commands |
| 3 To sound | 4 Confused | 5 Localizes pain |
| 2 To pressure | 3 Words | 4 Normal flexion (withdraws) |
| 1 None | 2 Sounds | 3 Abnormal flexion |
| | 1 None | 2 Extension |
| | | 1 None |

Total 3–15. Severe ≤ 8 (consider airway protection), moderate 9–12, mild 13–15. Report components (e.g. E3 V4 M6).

## Wells score — pulmonary embolism
| Criterion | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| Clinical signs of DVT | 3 |
| PE is the most likely diagnosis | 3 |
| Heart rate > 100 | 1.5 |
| Immobilization ≥ 3 days or surgery in previous 4 weeks | 1.5 |
| Previous DVT/PE | 1.5 |
| Hemoptysis | 1 |
| Active cancer | 1 |

Two-tier: ≤ 4 PE unlikely (D-dimer; if negative, PE excluded); > 4 PE likely (imaging, CTPA).
