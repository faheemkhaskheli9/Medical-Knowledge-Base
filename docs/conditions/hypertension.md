# Hypertension (adults)

**ICD-10:** I10 (essential/primary)

## Diagnosis — thresholds differ by guideline
| Guideline | Hypertension threshold (office) | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------------------|-------|
| AHA/ACC (US) | ≥ 130/80 mmHg (stage 1: 130–139/80–89; stage 2: ≥ 140/90) | Elevated: 120–129/< 80 |
| ESC 2024 (Europe) | ≥ 140/90 mmHg | Elevated BP: 120–139/70–89 |
| NICE (UK) | ≥ 140/90 clinic **and** ABPM/HBPM average ≥ 135/85 | Confirm with out-of-office readings |

- Confirm with repeated readings on separate occasions and out-of-office measurement (home or ambulatory) where possible.
- Check for secondary causes in young onset, resistant, or sudden-onset hypertension.

## Red flags
- **BP ≥ 180/120 with symptoms or organ damage** (chest pain, breathlessness, neurological deficit, visual loss, confusion, reduced urine output) → hypertensive emergency → emergency care.
- Severe hypertension in pregnancy, or with headache/visual symptoms → urgent assessment (pre-eclampsia).

## Management
- **Lifestyle:** sodium reduction, DASH-style diet, weight loss, physical activity, alcohol reduction, smoking cessation.
- **First-line drug classes:** ACE inhibitor or ARB, dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker (e.g. amlodipine), thiazide-like diuretic (chlorthalidone, indapamide).
- Most patients need ≥ 2 drugs; single-pill combinations improve adherence.
- Black African/Caribbean ancestry: CCB or thiazide-like diuretic preferred initially (NICE: CCB; AHA/ACC: CCB or thiazide).
- CKD with albuminuria, diabetes with albuminuria, HFrEF: include ACE inhibitor or ARB.
- **Do not** combine ACE inhibitor + ARB (or + aliskiren).
- Resistant hypertension (uncontrolled on 3 drugs incl. diuretic): check adherence and technique; add spironolactone if K⁺ allows.

## Targets
- AHA/ACC: < 130/80 mmHg for most adults.
- ESC 2024: systolic 120–129 mmHg if tolerated.
- Individualize for frailty, orthostatic hypotension, and limited life expectancy.

## Monitoring
Electrolytes and creatinine 1–2 weeks after starting/increasing ACE inhibitor, ARB, or diuretic; then periodically.

## Pregnancy
Avoid ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and direct renin inhibitors. Use labetalol, nifedipine, or methyldopa. See [Pregnancy and lactation](../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).
