# Amlodipine

**Class / ATC:** Dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker — C08CA01

## Dosing
| Population | Dose | Max |
|------------|------|-----|
| Adult (hypertension, angina) | 5 mg PO once daily | 10 mg/day |
| Older adults, hepatic impairment, small/frail | Start 2.5 mg once daily | 10 mg/day |

No renal adjustment. Full effect after 1–2 weeks.

## Cautions
Cardiogenic shock, severe aortic stenosis, unstable angina, severe hepatic impairment.

## Adverse effects
Dose-related **ankle edema** (not due to fluid overload — diuretics help little), flushing, headache, palpitations, gingival hyperplasia.

## Interactions
Simvastatin (max 20 mg/day with amlodipine); strong CYP3A4 inhibitors increase levels; additive hypotension with other antihypertensives.

## Pregnancy
Nifedipine is the preferred CCB in pregnancy with the most data; specialist advice for amlodipine.
