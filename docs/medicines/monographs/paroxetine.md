# Paroxetine

**Class:** Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) with strong CYP2D6 inhibition and anticholinergic activity. See also [sertraline](sertraline.md), [fluoxetine](fluoxetine.md) and [citalopram](citalopram.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA Paxil tablets/oral suspension)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Major depressive disorder | Start **20 mg PO once daily** (usually in the morning); increase in 10 mg/day steps at intervals of at least 1 week; maximum **50 mg per day** |
| Older or debilitated adults, severe renal or severe hepatic impairment | Start **10 mg once daily**; maximum **40 mg per day** |
| Other indications (OCD, panic disorder, social anxiety, GAD, PTSD) | Doses and maximums differ by indication — see label |
| Paediatric | Not approved in the US for children — see label and boxed warning |

- **Formulations are not interchangeable mg-for-mg:** paroxetine controlled-release (e.g. Paxil CR) has different starting doses and maximums; paroxetine mesylate 7.5 mg (Brisdelle) is for menopausal vasomotor symptoms only.

## Contraindications
- **MAOIs** (or within 14 days of stopping one); starting paroxetine in a patient on linezolid or IV methylene blue.
- **Thioridazine** or **pimozide** (QT prolongation).
- Hypersensitivity to paroxetine.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — suicidal thoughts and behaviours** in children, adolescents and young adults (under 25); monitor for worsening and emergence of suicidality, especially early in treatment and after dose changes. See [crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md).
- **Serotonin syndrome** with other serotonergic drugs.
- **Embryofetal toxicity:** first-trimester exposure is associated with an increased risk of cardiovascular malformations — see pregnancy section.
- **Discontinuation syndrome** (dizziness, sensory disturbances, irritability, anxiety): taper gradually; paroxetine's short half-life makes this more likely than with some SSRIs.
- Bleeding risk (with NSAIDs, aspirin, anticoagulants), hyponatraemia (SIADH, especially older adults), activation of mania, seizures, angle-closure glaucoma, bone fracture risk — see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| MAOIs, linezolid, IV methylene blue | Contraindicated |
| Thioridazine, pimozide | Contraindicated |
| Tamoxifen | Paroxetine (CYP2D6 inhibitor) may reduce tamoxifen efficacy — consider an antidepressant with little or no CYP2D6 inhibition |
| Drugs metabolised by CYP2D6 (e.g. some tricyclics, [propranolol](propranolol.md), metoprolol, atomoxetine, risperidone) | Raised levels — dose reduction may be needed |
| Other serotonergic drugs (triptans, tramadol, other antidepressants, St John's wort) | Serotonin syndrome risk |
| NSAIDs, [aspirin](aspirin.md), [warfarin](warfarin.md), DOACs | Increased bleeding risk |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) and [herb-drug interactions](../herb-drug-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: nausea, somnolence, insomnia, sweating, dry mouth, sexual dysfunction — see label.
- Serious: suicidality, serotonin syndrome, bleeding, hyponatraemia, mania, seizures — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Meta-analyses of epidemiological studies show an increased risk (less than 2-fold) of cardiovascular malformations after first-trimester exposure; late-pregnancy SSRI exposure carries neonatal adaptation and persistent pulmonary hypertension risks. Discuss with the prescriber before pregnancy or as soon as pregnancy is known — do not stop abruptly. See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Mood and suicidality (especially early and after dose changes); sodium in older adults or with diuretics; bleeding signs with antithrombotic drugs; withdrawal symptoms when stopping.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
It may take a few weeks to feel the full benefit. Do not stop suddenly — your prescriber will reduce the dose gradually. Tell your prescriber if you are pregnant or planning pregnancy, or take tamoxifen, other antidepressants, migraine medicines, tramadol, blood thinners or St John's wort. Seek help at once for thoughts of self-harm, or for agitation, high temperature, shaking and muscle twitching.
