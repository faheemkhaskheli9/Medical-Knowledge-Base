# Trazodone

**Class:** Antidepressant (labelled in the US for major depressive disorder). Often used off-label at low doses for insomnia — off-label dosing is not covered here.

## Dosing (adult, FDA, major depressive disorder)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | Start 150 mg daily in divided doses; may increase by 50 mg/day every 3–4 days; maximum 400 mg/day in divided doses |
| Paediatric | Not covered here — see label (boxed warning applies to young patients) |
| Renal impairment, hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

- Some older labels describe higher limits for hospitalised patients — see label.

## Contraindications
- **MAOIs:** do not use trazodone with an MAOI or within 14 days of stopping an MAOI; allow at least 14 days after stopping trazodone before starting an MAOI.
- Other contraindications: see label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — suicidal thoughts and behaviours:** antidepressants increased the risk in paediatric and young adult patients in short-term studies; monitor all patients for clinical worsening and emergence of suicidal thoughts. See [crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md) and [red flags](../../guidelines/red-flags.md).
- **QT prolongation:** avoid in patients with known QT prolongation and in combination with CYP3A4 inhibitors or other QT-prolonging drugs.
- **Priapism:** painful, prolonged erections; delayed treatment can cause permanent damage. Stop and seek emergency care for a prolonged erection (labels differ: more than 4 hours in some, 6 hours in others — use the product label).

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| MAOIs | Contraindicated (14-day gap in both directions) |
| CYP3A4 inhibitors and other drugs that prolong the QT interval | QT prolongation — avoid the combination (check each drug's monograph or label) |
| Other interactions | See label and [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) |

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: suicidal thoughts in young patients, QT prolongation and arrhythmia, priapism.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Mood and suicidal thoughts, especially early in treatment and after dose changes; ECG in patients with cardiac risk factors or interacting drugs; interacting medicines.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care and cardiac monitoring — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Tell your prescriber about all your medicines, especially other antidepressants (MAOIs), antibiotics, antifungals and heart-rhythm medicines. Seek emergency care for an erection lasting several hours, fainting or a racing/irregular heartbeat. Contact your prescriber or a crisis service straight away if you have thoughts of harming yourself.
