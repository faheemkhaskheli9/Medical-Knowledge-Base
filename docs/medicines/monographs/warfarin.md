# Warfarin

**Class / ATC:** Vitamin K antagonist — B01AA03 — **high-alert medication**

## Target INR
| Indication | Target INR |
|------------|-----------|
| Most indications (VTE, AF, bioprosthetic valve) | 2.0–3.0 (target 2.5) |
| Mechanical mitral valve or mechanical valve with additional risk factors | 2.5–3.5 (target 3.0) |

Dosing is individualized by INR; consider lower starting doses in older adults, low body weight, liver disease, heart failure, interacting drugs, or known CYP2C9/VKORC1 variants.

## Major interactions
| Effect | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| ↑ INR / bleeding | Amiodarone, fluconazole, metronidazole, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, macrolides, fluoroquinolones, NSAIDs, antiplatelets, SSRIs |
| ↓ INR | Rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin, St John's wort, large changes in vitamin K intake |

## Reversal
- Elevated INR without bleeding: hold dose(s) ± oral vitamin K per guideline thresholds.
- Major bleeding: hold warfarin, IV vitamin K, plus 4-factor prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC).

## Pregnancy
Teratogenic (warfarin embryopathy); generally avoid — use LMWH. Mechanical valve in pregnancy requires specialist management. Compatible with breastfeeding.
