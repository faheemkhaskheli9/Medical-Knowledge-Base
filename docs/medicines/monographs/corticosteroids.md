# Systemic Corticosteroids (prednisolone)

**ATC:** H02AB — prednisolone H02AB06, prednisone H02AB07, dexamethasone H02AB02, hydrocortisone H02AB09

## Equivalent anti-inflammatory doses
| Steroid | Equivalent dose |
|---------|-----------------|
| Hydrocortisone | 20 mg |
| Prednisolone / prednisone | 5 mg |
| Methylprednisolone | 4 mg |
| Dexamethasone | 0.75 mg |

Hydrocortisone has significant mineralocorticoid activity; dexamethasone has almost none.

## Use
Take once daily in the **morning with food**. Dose depends on indication (e.g. asthma exacerbation, COPD exacerbation, autoimmune disease).

## When to taper (BNF — gradual withdrawal advised if any)
- More than 40 mg prednisolone (or equivalent) daily for > 1 week
- Treatment for > 3 weeks
- Repeated evening doses, or recent repeated courses
- Other causes of adrenal suppression

Short courses (< 3 weeks, ≤ 40 mg) can usually be stopped abruptly.

## Adrenal insufficiency risk
- Long-term users need a **steroid emergency card**; **sick-day rules** — increase dose during febrile illness per instructions; never stop suddenly.
- Vomiting/unable to take oral steroid → emergency IM/IV hydrocortisone.

## Adverse effects
Hyperglycemia, infection risk (and masked symptoms), mood change/psychosis, insomnia, hypertension, fluid retention, osteoporosis, myopathy, cataract/glaucoma, peptic ulcer (especially with NSAIDs), skin thinning, weight gain, growth suppression in children.

## Prevention
- **Bone protection** for courses ≥ 3 months (calcium/vitamin D; bisphosphonate per fracture risk).
- Gastroprotection with concurrent NSAID/anticoagulant.
- Monitor glucose and BP; avoid live vaccines on high-dose therapy.
