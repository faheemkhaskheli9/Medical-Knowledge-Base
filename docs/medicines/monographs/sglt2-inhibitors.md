# SGLT2 inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin)

**Class / ATC:** Sodium–glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors — empagliflozin A10BK03, dapagliflozin A10BK01. Others: canagliflozin, ertugliflozin.

**Mechanism:** Block glucose (and sodium) reabsorption in the proximal tubule — glycosuria, mild natriuresis.

## Dosing (adult, oral, once daily)
| Indication | Empagliflozin | Dapagliflozin |
|------------|---------------|---------------|
| Type 2 diabetes (glycemic control) | 10 mg; may increase to 25 mg | 10 mg (US label: start 5 mg, increase to 10 mg) |
| Heart failure (reduced or preserved EF) | 10 mg | 10 mg |
| Chronic kidney disease | 10 mg | 10 mg |

- Glucose-lowering effect falls as eGFR declines, but heart and kidney benefits persist. Label thresholds for **starting** in CKD: empagliflozin eGFR ≥ 20, dapagliflozin eGFR ≥ 25 mL/min/1.73 m²; may usually continue below these until dialysis. Check the current label for glycemic-control thresholds.
- Not licensed for type 1 diabetes in most regions (DKA risk).

## Contraindications
Serious hypersensitivity. Not recommended on dialysis (no established benefit), in type 1 diabetes, or for treatment of DKA.

## Warnings
- **Diabetic ketoacidosis**, including **euglycemic DKA** (glucose may be near normal). Higher risk with acute illness, reduced food intake, surgery, alcohol excess, insulin dose reduction. FDA: stop at least **3 days before scheduled surgery** (canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, empagliflozin; 4 days for ertugliflozin).
- **Sick-day rule:** pause during vomiting, diarrhea or dehydrating illness; restart 24–48 h after eating and drinking normally.
- Genital fungal infections (common); urinary tract infections, including urosepsis.
- **Fournier's gangrene** (necrotizing fasciitis of the perineum) — rare, urgent.
- Volume depletion and hypotension, especially with loop diuretics and in older adults.
- An initial small eGFR dip after starting is expected and is not a reason to stop on its own.
- Canagliflozin: lower-limb amputation signal in CANVAS.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Insulin, sulfonylureas | Hypoglycemia — consider reducing their dose when starting |
| Loop and thiazide diuretics | Volume depletion, hypotension |

## Adverse effects
- Common: genital mycotic infection, polyuria, thirst, UTI.
- Serious: DKA, Fournier's gangrene, volume depletion with AKI.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Not recommended in the second and third trimesters (animal data: renal development); avoid in pregnancy and breastfeeding — switch to insulin for diabetes in pregnancy.

## Monitoring
eGFR before starting and periodically; volume status and blood pressure; ketones (blood preferred) if unwell, with nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain or breathlessness — even if glucose is normal.

## Patient counselling
Expect more urination; keep hydrated. Keep the genital area clean; report itching or discharge. Stop and seek urgent care for vomiting, abdominal pain, fast breathing or unusual drowsiness (possible DKA), or pain, swelling or redness of the genitals or perineum with fever. Follow sick-day rules and tell the surgical team before an operation. See [type 2 diabetes](../../conditions/type-2-diabetes.md).
