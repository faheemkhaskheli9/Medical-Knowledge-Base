# Doxycycline

**Class / ATC:** Tetracycline — J01AA02. WHO AWaRe **Access** group.

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Most infections (CAP, chlamydia, cellulitis with MRSA risk) | 100 mg every 12 h (200 mg on day 1 in some indications) |
| Chlamydia | 100 mg twice daily for 7 days |
| Malaria prophylaxis | 100 mg daily, from 1–2 days before travel until 4 weeks after leaving |
| Lyme disease prophylaxis (eligible tick bite) | 200 mg single dose |

## How to take
With a full glass of water, sitting or standing upright for 30 minutes, **not at bedtime** — prevents esophageal ulceration. Separate from antacids, iron, calcium, zinc, and magnesium by 2–3 hours.

## Warnings
- **Photosensitivity** — sun protection.
- Esophagitis/esophageal ulcer.
- Intracranial hypertension (rare).
- Tooth discoloration with prolonged/repeated use in young children; short courses (≤ 21 days) acceptable at any age for serious infections (AAP).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Avoid in pregnancy (especially from the second trimester). Short courses generally acceptable while breastfeeding.

## Interactions
Reduced effect with antacids/minerals and enzyme inducers (carbamazepine, phenytoin, rifampicin); may increase warfarin effect; avoid with retinoids (intracranial hypertension).
