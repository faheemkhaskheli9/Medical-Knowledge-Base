# Finasteride (5-alpha-reductase inhibitors)

**Class / ATC:** 5-alpha-reductase (type II) inhibitor — G04CB01 (D11AX10 for hair loss). Other: dutasteride (types I and II). Lowers dihydrotestosterone (DHT), shrinking the prostate over months. Often combined with an alpha-blocker ([tamsulosin](tamsulosin.md)) for symptomatic benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH).

## Dosing (oral, adult men)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| BPH (Proscar) | 5 mg once daily, with or without food. Benefit may take 6 months or more |
| Male pattern hair loss (Propecia) | 1 mg once daily (UK: men aged 18–41) |
| Renal impairment, older adults | No adjustment (label) |
| Hepatic impairment | Use with caution (extensively metabolized by the liver) |

Not indicated in women or children.

## Contraindications
- **Women who are or may become pregnant** (risk of abnormal genital development in a male fetus).
- Children.
- Hypersensitivity.

## Warnings
- **PSA:** finasteride lowers serum PSA by about 50% within 6 months. Set a new baseline after at least 6 months; **double the PSA value** before comparing with normal ranges for untreated men. Any confirmed rise on treatment needs evaluation; the free-to-total PSA ratio is unaffected.
- **High-grade prostate cancer:** may be increased (PCPT trial: Gleason 8–10 in 1.8% on finasteride vs 1.1% on placebo). Not approved for prostate cancer prevention. Rule out prostate cancer and urinary obstruction before treating BPH.
- **Depression, suicidal thoughts and sexual dysfunction** (reduced libido, erectile and ejaculatory problems) — which **may persist after stopping** (MHRA). Ask about mood history before starting; stop and seek advice if mood changes.
- **Handling:** women who are or may be pregnant should **not handle crushed or broken tablets**; intact coated tablets are safe to touch.
- Breast changes (enlargement, tenderness, lump or nipple discharge) — report; male breast cancer has been reported.

## Major interactions
No clinically important interactions identified in labeling. Dutasteride or another 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor should not be combined with it.

## Adverse effects
- Common: reduced libido, erectile dysfunction, ejaculation disorders (reduced volume), breast tenderness or enlargement.
- Serious: depression, suicidal ideation, persistent sexual dysfunction, hypersensitivity (rash, angioedema), high-grade prostate cancer (above).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Contraindicated in women. Finasteride is present in small amounts in semen; the label states this is not expected to harm a male fetus, but some product information advises condom use if the partner is or may be pregnant — check the local SmPC. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Urinary symptoms; PSA (interpret as above) and prostate examination per urology guidance; mood and sexual function.

## Overdose / antidote
Single doses well above the therapeutic dose have been taken without adverse effects in studies. Supportive care ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Take once a day; it can take 6 months or more to work. Tell anyone checking your PSA that you take finasteride. Pregnant women should not handle broken or crushed tablets. Stop and talk to your doctor if you feel low, have thoughts of self-harm ([crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md)), or notice breast lumps or nipple discharge. Report sexual side effects, which can occasionally continue after stopping.
