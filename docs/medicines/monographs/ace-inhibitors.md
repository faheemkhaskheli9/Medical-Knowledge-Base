# ACE Inhibitors (lisinopril, ramipril, enalapril)

**Class / ATC:** C09AA

**Uses:** Hypertension, heart failure (HFrEF), post-MI, CKD with albuminuria, diabetic kidney disease.

## Typical adult dosing (hypertension)
| Drug | Start | Usual maintenance | Max |
|------|-------|-------------------|-----|
| Lisinopril | 10 mg once daily (5 mg if on diuretic or renal impairment) | 20–40 mg daily | 80 mg/day |
| Ramipril | 2.5 mg once daily | 2.5–10 mg daily | 10 mg/day (hypertension) |
| Enalapril | 5 mg once daily (2.5 mg if on diuretic) | 10–40 mg/day in 1–2 doses | 40 mg/day |

Heart-failure doses start lower and are titrated to target.

## Contraindications
- History of **angioedema** (any cause related to ACE inhibitor, hereditary/idiopathic).
- **Pregnancy** (fetotoxic) — switch before conception.
- With **sacubitril/valsartan** (36-hour washout); with aliskiren in diabetes.
- Bilateral renal artery stenosis (or stenosis in a single functioning kidney).
- Do not combine ACE inhibitor + ARB.

## Adverse effects
Dry cough (~10%; switch to ARB), hyperkalemia, acute kidney injury, first-dose hypotension, angioedema (more common in people of Black African ancestry; can occur after years of use).

## Monitoring
K⁺ and creatinine before starting and 1–2 weeks after starting/increasing dose. A creatinine rise up to ~30% is usually acceptable; larger rises — review (renal artery stenosis, volume depletion, NSAIDs).

## Sick-day rule
Pause during dehydrating illness (vomiting, diarrhea, fever); restart 24–48 h after eating and drinking normally.
