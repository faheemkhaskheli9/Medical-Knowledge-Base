# Ezetimibe

**Class:** Cholesterol absorption inhibitor (acts at the NPC1L1 transporter in the small intestine). Not a statin — see [atorvastatin](atorvastatin.md), [rosuvastatin](rosuvastatin.md) and [simvastatin](simvastatin.md) for statins, which it is often combined with.

## Dosing (adult, FDA Zetia label)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | **10 mg PO once daily**, with or without food (alone, with a statin, or with fenofibrate) |
| With a bile acid sequestrant (e.g. colestyramine, colesevelam) | Give ezetimibe at least 2 hours before or at least 4 hours after the sequestrant |
| Renal impairment | No dose adjustment |
| Mild hepatic impairment | No dose adjustment |
| Moderate or severe hepatic impairment | **Not recommended** |
| Paediatric, older adults | See label |

- Fixed-dose combinations with statins (e.g. ezetimibe/simvastatin) carry the statin's own dose limits and interactions — check that product's label.

## Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to ezetimibe.
- **With a statin:** active liver disease or unexplained persistent raised transaminases; pregnancy and breastfeeding wording follows the statin's label.
- With fenofibrate: wherever fenofibrate (or a statin/other LDL-lowering therapy being combined) is contraindicated.
- Others: see label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- No boxed warning.
- **Liver enzymes:** transaminase rises were more frequent with ezetimibe plus a statin than with a statin alone — check liver tests as the statin label directs.
- **Myopathy/rhabdomyolysis** has been reported (mostly with a statin or fibrate); stop if CK is markedly raised or myopathy is suspected — see label.
- **Gallstones with fenofibrate:** both may increase cholesterol excretion into bile. If cholelithiasis is suspected, do gallbladder studies and consider alternative therapy.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Ciclosporin (cyclosporine) | Exposure to both drugs increases — monitor ciclosporin concentrations; use caution |
| Bile acid sequestrants | Reduce ezetimibe absorption — separate doses (see table) |
| Fibrates other than fenofibrate (e.g. gemfibrozil) | Co-administration not recommended (not adequately studied) |
| Fenofibrate | Gallstone risk (see warnings) |
| [Warfarin](warfarin.md) | Monitor INR when ezetimibe is added — see label |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label (e.g. upper respiratory infection, diarrhoea, arthralgia were reported in trials).
- Serious: hypersensitivity (including angioedema), myopathy/rhabdomyolysis, hepatitis, pancreatitis, cholelithiasis — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Limited human data — see label (and the statin label when combined) and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Lipids per guideline; liver tests when used with a statin (as the statin label directs); muscle symptoms; ciclosporin levels if co-prescribed; INR if on warfarin.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take one tablet once a day, with or without food. If you also take colestyramine or colesevelam, take ezetimibe at least 2 hours before or 4 hours after it. Keep taking your statin if one is prescribed with it. Report unexplained muscle pain or weakness, yellow skin or eyes, or severe stomach pain.
