# Clindamycin

**Class:** Lincosamide antibacterial (related to lincomycin). Oral capsules, oral solution, injection, and topical/vaginal products. Choose empiric therapy with [antimicrobial stewardship](../../infections/antimicrobial-stewardship.md) and local guidance.

## Dosing (adult, oral capsules, FDA)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult, serious infections | 150–300 mg every 6 hours |
| Adult, more severe infections | 300–450 mg every 6 hours |
| Paediatric, renal impairment, hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

- Take capsules with a full glass of water to avoid oesophageal irritation (one generic label: 6–8 ounces of water and stay upright for at least 30 minutes).
- Cleocin HCl capsules come as 75 mg, 150 mg and 300 mg; some generics only 150 mg and 300 mg.
- Injection and topical/vaginal products have separate dosing — see label.

## Contraindications
- History of hypersensitivity to clindamycin or lincomycin.
- Topical and vaginal products also list a history of regional enteritis, ulcerative colitis or antibiotic-associated colitis.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — *Clostridioides difficile*-associated diarrhoea (CDAD):** ranges from mild diarrhoea to fatal colitis; oral and parenteral clindamycin have been associated with severe colitis that may result in death. Stop clindamycin if significant diarrhoea develops and assess for *C. difficile*.
- **Severe hypersensitivity and skin reactions:** toxic epidermal necrolysis, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, and drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms (DRESS), some fatal. See [allergy and cross-reactivity](../../allergy/cross-reactivity.md).
- **Neuromuscular blocking properties:** may enhance other neuromuscular blocking agents — use with caution.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Strong CYP3A4 inducers (for example rifampicin/rifampin) | Lower clindamycin levels — monitor for loss of effectiveness |
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors | Monitor for adverse reactions |
| Neuromuscular blocking agents | Enhanced neuromuscular blockade — use with caution |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: *C. difficile* colitis, SJS/TEN, DRESS, exfoliative dermatitis (rare), oesophageal irritation if swallowed without enough water.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Bowel habit during and after treatment; skin reactions; clinical response, especially with CYP3A4 inducers. Long courses: see label.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Swallow capsules with a full glass of water and stay upright afterwards. Contact a doctor promptly for diarrhoea — especially if watery, bloody, or with stomach cramps or fever, including after the course has finished. Stop and seek urgent help for a blistering or peeling rash, facial swelling, or fever with rash.
