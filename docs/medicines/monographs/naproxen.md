# Naproxen

**Class:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). See also [ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md) for shared NSAID points.

## Dosing (adult, FDA Naprosyn tablets)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, ankylosing spondylitis | 250 mg or 500 mg twice daily (suspension: 250 mg, 375 mg or 500 mg twice daily). Patients who tolerate lower doses well may be increased to 1500 mg/day for up to 6 months |
| Acute gout | 750 mg, then 250 mg every 8 hours until the attack subsides |
| Renal impairment | Not recommended in moderate to severe and severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min) |
| All patients | Lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with treatment goals |

- Dosing sections differ by formulation (tablets, suspension, naproxen sodium, delayed-release) — check the label for the product used.
- Paediatric, hepatic impairment, older adults: see label.

## Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to naproxen.
- History of asthma, urticaria or other allergic-type reactions after aspirin or other NSAIDs (aspirin-sensitive asthma).
- **In the setting of coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) surgery.**
- Labels also list prior serious skin reactions to NSAIDs — check the specific product label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — cardiovascular:** increased risk of serious thrombotic events (myocardial infarction, stroke), which can be fatal; may occur early in treatment and may increase with duration of use.
- **Boxed warning — gastrointestinal:** bleeding, ulceration and perforation of the stomach or intestines, which can be fatal; can occur at any time and without warning symptoms.
- Asthma without aspirin sensitivity: monitor.
- See [renal and hepatic dosing](../../special-populations/renal-hepatic.md) and [older adults](../../special-populations/older-adults.md).

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Warfarin, other anticoagulants and antiplatelets, aspirin, SSRIs/SNRIs | Increased bleeding risk — monitor. Analgesic-dose aspirin with naproxen is generally not recommended; aspirin does not consistently mitigate NSAID cardiovascular risk and adds GI risk |
| ACE inhibitors, ARBs | Reduced antihypertensive effect — monitor blood pressure |
| Loop and thiazide diuretics | Reduced natriuretic effect — monitor renal function and diuretic effect |
| [Lithium](lithium.md) | Raised plasma lithium, reduced renal lithium clearance — monitor for lithium toxicity |
| [Methotrexate](methotrexate.md) | Increased methotrexate toxicity (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, renal dysfunction) — monitor |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: GI bleeding, ulceration and perforation; myocardial infarction and stroke; renal impairment; hypersensitivity reactions; serious skin reactions.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Avoid NSAIDs, including naproxen, from about 30 weeks of gestation. Between about 20 and 30 weeks, limit use (risk of oligohydramnios and fetal renal dysfunction). See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Blood pressure, renal function (especially with ACE inhibitors, ARBs or diuretics), signs of GI bleeding, lithium levels or methotrexate toxicity when combined.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Use the lowest dose for the shortest time. Do not combine with other NSAIDs (including over-the-counter ibuprofen or aspirin for pain). Seek urgent care for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, chest pain, weakness on one side, or slurred speech. Tell your prescriber if you are pregnant, have kidney or heart disease, or take blood thinners, lithium, methotrexate, or blood-pressure tablets.
