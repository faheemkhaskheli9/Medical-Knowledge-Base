# Atorvastatin

**Class / ATC:** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (statin) — C10AA05

## Dosing
| Intensity | Dose (once daily, any time of day) |
|-----------|------------------------------------|
| Moderate | 10–20 mg |
| High | 40–80 mg |

No renal dose adjustment.

## Contraindications
Active liver disease or unexplained persistent transaminase elevation; hypersensitivity. **Pregnancy:** FDA (2021) removed the blanket contraindication but advises stopping in most pregnant patients; not recommended while breastfeeding.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Action |
|------|--------|
| Clarithromycin, itraconazole, some HIV/HCV protease inhibitors | Limit atorvastatin dose (often ≤ 20 mg) or avoid — check label |
| Ciclosporin, tipranavir/ritonavir, gemfibrozil | Avoid |
| Fusidic acid (systemic) | Fatal rhabdomyolysis reported — withhold statin |
| Grapefruit juice (large amounts) | Increased levels |

## Adverse effects
Muscle symptoms (myalgia; rare myopathy/rhabdomyolysis — check CK if symptomatic), raised transaminases, small increase in new-onset diabetes.

## Monitoring
Baseline lipids and ALT; repeat lipids 4–12 weeks after starting/changing dose, then periodically.
