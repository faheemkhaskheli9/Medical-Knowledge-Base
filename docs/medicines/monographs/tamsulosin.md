# Tamsulosin (alpha-blockers)

**Class / ATC:** Selective alpha-1A adrenoceptor antagonist — G04CA02. Others: alfuzosin, doxazosin, terazosin.

## Dosing (adult men, oral)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) — US | 0.4 mg once daily, about 30 minutes after the same meal each day; may increase to 0.8 mg once daily after 2–4 weeks if response is inadequate (FDA) |
| BPH — UK | 400 micrograms (0.4 mg) once daily, modified-release (BNF) |
| Restarting after several days' interruption | Restart at 0.4 mg once daily (FDA) |

- Use for helping ureteric stones pass (medical expulsive therapy) is off-label in many countries.
- Not studied in CrCl < 10 mL/min or severe hepatic impairment.
- Rule out prostate cancer and other causes of urinary symptoms before starting (NICE CG97); see [red flags](../../guidelines/red-flags.md) for urinary retention.

## Contraindications
Hypersensitivity. UK SmPC also lists a history of orthostatic hypotension and severe hepatic impairment.

## Warnings
- **Orthostatic hypotension, dizziness, syncope** — especially after the first dose or a dose increase; risk with driving or operating machinery.
- **Intraoperative floppy iris syndrome** during cataract or glaucoma surgery — tell the eye surgeon before surgery, including about past use.
- Priapism (rare) — emergency.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors** (ketoconazole, itraconazole, clarithromycin, ritonavir) | Raised tamsulosin levels — FDA: do not combine. Caution with moderate CYP3A4 or strong CYP2D6 inhibitors |
| Other alpha-blockers | Hypotension — do not combine |
| PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil), other antihypertensives | Additive hypotension |

## Adverse effects
- Common: dizziness, ejaculation disorders (reduced or absent ejaculate), rhinitis, headache.
- Serious: syncope, priapism, floppy iris syndrome, angioedema (rare).

## Older adults
Higher risk of falls from postural hypotension ([older adults](../../special-populations/older-adults.md)).

## Patient counselling
Swallow capsules whole — do not crush, chew or open. Take at the same time each day after a meal. Stand up slowly, especially at first; sit or lie down if dizzy. Tell your eye surgeon you take or have taken tamsulosin before cataract surgery. Seek urgent help for a painful erection lasting more than 4 hours or inability to pass urine.
