# Methotrexate (low-dose, once weekly)

**Class / ATC:** Antimetabolite (folate antagonist), conventional synthetic DMARD — L04AX03 (immunosuppressant use), L01BA01 (oncology). This page covers low-dose oral use for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis and similar conditions; oncology regimens are specialist-only.

> **High-alert medicine** — fatal toxicity when taken **daily instead of weekly**. See [high-alert medications](../high-alert-medications.md).

## Dosing (adult, oral, **once weekly on the same day**)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Rheumatoid arthritis | Start 7.5 mg once weekly; adjust by specialist according to response; max 25 mg once weekly (BNF) |
| Psoriasis | 10–25 mg once weekly (FDA label); titrated by specialist |
| Folic acid co-prescription | Reduces mucosal, GI and liver adverse effects — BNF: folic acid 5 mg once weekly on a **different** day from methotrexate; regimens vary by country |

UK safety advice: use a single tablet strength (usually 2.5 mg) to avoid confusion with 10 mg tablets; record the day of the week on the prescription.

## Contraindications
Pregnancy (non-oncology use) and breastfeeding; significant hepatic impairment, alcohol dependence or chronic liver disease; pre-existing blood dyscrasias (bone marrow hypoplasia, leucopenia, thrombocytopenia, significant anemia); immunodeficiency; active serious infection. Renal impairment raises levels (renally excreted) — reduce dose or avoid according to the label/BNF.

## Warnings (FDA boxed warning, summarized)
- Serious, sometimes fatal toxicity: **myelosuppression**, **hepatotoxicity** (fibrosis, cirrhosis), **pneumonitis**, GI toxicity (mucositis, diarrhea), serious infections, severe skin reactions.
- Embryo-fetal toxicity — use effective contraception; plan conception with the specialist (label/BNF give washout periods).
- Third-space fluid (ascites, pleural effusion) and dehydration delay elimination and increase toxicity.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Trimethoprim, co-trimoxazole | Additive folate antagonism — bone marrow suppression, deaths reported. **Avoid** |
| NSAIDs, aspirin, penicillins, probenecid | Reduced renal excretion — raised methotrexate levels (most important at high doses; monitor at low doses) |
| Proton pump inhibitors | Delayed methotrexate clearance (mainly high-dose regimens) |
| Other hepatotoxic drugs (e.g. leflunomide), alcohol | Additive liver toxicity |
| Live vaccines | Avoid during treatment (immunosuppression) |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: nausea, mouth ulcers, fatigue, raised liver enzymes, hair thinning.
- Serious: pancytopenia, hepatic fibrosis, pneumonitis (new cough or breathlessness), severe infections, mucositis.

## Monitoring (BSR/BHPR 2017)
Baseline: FBC, creatinine/eGFR, ALT and/or AST, albumin; chest X-ray if not done recently; hepatitis B, hepatitis C and HIV screening.
Then FBC, creatinine/eGFR, ALT/AST and albumin **every 2 weeks** until on a stable dose for 6 weeks; then **monthly for 3 months**; then **at least every 12 weeks**. Repeat more often after a dose increase or in renal impairment.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Contraindicated in pregnancy for non-oncology use (teratogenic and abortifacient). Avoid breastfeeding. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Overdose / antidote
Folinic acid (calcium folinate/leucovorin) as early as possible; glucarpidase for high-dose toxicity with delayed clearance. Contact a poison center. See [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling
Take **once a week, on the same day** — never daily. Do not double up a missed dose without advice. Keep blood test appointments. Seek urgent advice for sore throat, fever, mouth ulcers, unexplained bruising or bleeding, new cough or breathlessness. Limit alcohol. Tell any prescriber or pharmacist you take methotrexate, and avoid over-the-counter NSAIDs and trimethoprim unless they approve.
