# Bupropion

**Class / ATC:** Norepinephrine-dopamine reuptake inhibitor (aminoketone antidepressant; smoking-cessation aid) — N06AX12. Not an SSRI/SNRI; compare [sertraline](sertraline.md), [venlafaxine](venlafaxine.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA)
| Indication / product | Dose |
|----------------------|------|
| Depression — extended-release (XL) | 150 mg once daily in the morning; after 4 days may increase to the usual target of 300 mg once daily; maximum 450 mg daily |
| Smoking cessation — sustained-release (Zyban) | 150 mg once daily for 3 days, then 150 mg twice daily (at least 8 hours apart); maximum 300 mg daily. Start while still smoking and set a quit date within the first 2 weeks; treat for 7–12 weeks |
| Moderate to severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh 7–15) | Maximum 150 mg every other day (XL) |
| Mild hepatic impairment or renal impairment (GFR below 90 mL/min) | Consider reducing the dose and/or frequency |

- Swallow tablets whole — do not crush, split or chew (higher seizure risk).
- XL, SR and IR products are **not interchangeable mg-for-mg in frequency**; check the product.

## Contraindications
- Seizure disorder.
- Current or prior bulimia or anorexia nervosa (higher seizure incidence).
- Abrupt withdrawal of alcohol, benzodiazepines, barbiturates or antiepileptic drugs.
- MAOIs for psychiatric disorders taken together or within 14 days of each other; starting in patients on linezolid or IV methylene blue.
- Hypersensitivity.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Suicidal thoughts and behaviors** (boxed warning) in children, adolescents and young adults on antidepressants; monitor for worsening and emergent suicidality. Neuropsychiatric reactions have also been reported in people using it to stop smoking. See [crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md).
- **Seizures:** dose-related. Do not exceed the maximum dose, titrate gradually, and stop permanently if a seizure occurs. Extra caution with other seizure risk factors (head injury, CNS tumour, severe hepatic impairment, drugs that lower seizure threshold, excess alcohol, stimulants).
- **Hypertension:** can raise blood pressure, including with nicotine replacement — monitor.
- **Activation of mania/hypomania;** screen for bipolar disorder. **Angle-closure glaucoma** in susceptible patients.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| MAOIs (including linezolid, IV methylene blue) | Hypertensive reactions — contraindicated |
| CYP2D6 substrates (e.g. many antidepressants, antipsychotics, beta-blockers such as metoprolol, flecainide), tamoxifen (needs CYP2D6 activation), codeine/tramadol ([opioids](opioids.md)) | Bupropion inhibits CYP2D6 — substrate levels rise; prodrugs may work less well |
| CYP2B6 inducers (e.g. carbamazepine, rifampicin, ritonavir) | Lower bupropion levels |
| Drugs that lower seizure threshold (antipsychotics, antidepressants, theophylline, systemic corticosteroids, tramadol) | Additive seizure risk |
| Dopaminergic drugs (levodopa, amantadine) | More CNS adverse effects |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: insomnia, dry mouth, headache, nausea, agitation, tremor.
- Serious: seizures, neuropsychiatric effects, hypertension, mania, severe allergic reactions.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Specialist advice; weigh untreated depression or continued smoking against risk. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Mood and suicidality (especially early and after dose changes), blood pressure, seizure risk factors, interacting CYP2D6 drugs.

## Overdose / antidote
Seizures (sometimes delayed with modified-release products), tachycardia, arrhythmias; no specific antidote — supportive care, benzodiazepines for seizures ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Take in the morning (XL) and never take an extra dose to catch up — this increases seizure risk. Swallow whole. Limit alcohol. Tell your prescriber about seizures, eating disorders, head injury or heavy drinking. Seek help urgently for a seizure, or for thoughts of self-harm or unusual changes in mood or behaviour.
