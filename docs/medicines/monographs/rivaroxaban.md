# Rivaroxaban

**Class / ATC:** Direct oral anticoagulant, factor Xa inhibitor — B01AF01 — **high-alert medication** ([high-alert list](../high-alert-medications.md)). Compare [apixaban](apixaban.md) (twice daily) and [warfarin](warfarin.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA unless stated)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Non-valvular atrial fibrillation | CrCl > 50 mL/min: 20 mg once daily **with the evening meal**. CrCl ≤ 50 mL/min: 15 mg once daily with the evening meal |
| DVT/PE treatment | 15 mg twice daily **with food** for the first 21 days, then 20 mg once daily with food |
| Prophylaxis after hip replacement | 10 mg once daily, with or without food, for 35 days; first dose 6–10 hours after surgery once hemostasis is established |
| Prophylaxis after knee replacement | 10 mg once daily for 12 days (EU SmPC: 2 weeks) |
| CAD or PAD (vascular protection) | 2.5 mg twice daily, combined with low-dose [aspirin](aspirin.md) per label |

- **Food:** the 15 mg and 20 mg doses must be taken with food (absorption is reduced on an empty stomach); 10 mg can be taken with or without food.
- **Renal:** DVT/PE treatment — avoid if CrCl < 15 mL/min (FDA). The EU SmPC uses CrCl 15–49 mL/min (AF: 15 mg once daily) and does not recommend use below 15 mL/min. Use the Cockcroft-Gault CrCl, not eGFR, for DOAC dosing ([formulas](../../clinical-reference/formulas.md)).
- Hepatic impairment: avoid in moderate or severe impairment (Child-Pugh B or C) and in liver disease with coagulopathy (FDA); EU SmPC contraindicates hepatic disease with coagulopathy and clinically relevant bleeding risk, including cirrhosis Child-Pugh B and C.
- Children: weight-based oral suspension per label — specialist use.

## Contraindications
- Active pathological bleeding.
- Severe hypersensitivity to rivaroxaban.
- EU SmPC also: lesions or conditions with a significant risk of major bleeding; hepatic disease with coagulopathy (above); pregnancy and breastfeeding.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Premature discontinuation** increases the risk of thrombotic events — if stopped for a reason other than pathological bleeding or completing a course, consider cover with another anticoagulant (boxed warning).
- **Spinal/epidural hematoma** with neuraxial anesthesia or spinal puncture, which may cause long-term or permanent paralysis (boxed warning). Follow timing rules for catheter removal and monitor neurological signs.
- **Prosthetic heart valves:** not recommended.
- **Triple-positive antiphospholipid syndrome:** not recommended (increased thrombosis).
- Bleeding risk rises with antiplatelets, NSAIDs ([ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md)), SSRIs/SNRIs and other anticoagulants.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Combined P-gp **and** strong CYP3A inhibitors (e.g. ketoconazole, ritonavir) | Raise rivaroxaban levels — avoid (FDA) |
| Combined P-gp and strong CYP3A **inducers** (e.g. [carbamazepine](carbamazepine.md), [phenytoin](phenytoin.md), rifampicin, St John's wort) | Lower levels, risk of stroke or VTE — avoid (FDA) |
| Aspirin, clopidogrel, NSAIDs, other anticoagulants | Additive bleeding risk |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: bleeding (bruising, nosebleeds, gum or menstrual bleeding), anemia.
- Serious: major or fatal bleeding (gastrointestinal, intracranial), spinal/epidural hematoma.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Contraindicated in pregnancy and breastfeeding in the EU SmPC; the FDA label advises weighing bleeding risk (including obstetric hemorrhage) and fetal risk. LMWH ([enoxaparin](enoxaparin.md)) is usually preferred in pregnancy. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Renal function (at baseline and at least yearly, more often in older adults or CKD), hemoglobin, liver function, adherence, bleeding. Routine coagulation monitoring is not needed; standard INR does not reflect rivaroxaban effect.

## Overdose / antidote
Major bleeding: stop the drug, supportive care; reversal with **andexanet alfa** or 4-factor prothrombin complex concentrate, depending on local availability ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)). Not removed by dialysis (highly protein bound).

## Patient counselling points
Take 15 mg and 20 mg tablets with a meal. Do not stop without talking to your prescriber — stopping suddenly raises stroke and clot risk. If a once-daily dose is missed, take it the same day; do not double the next day. Tell every doctor, dentist and pharmacist you take an anticoagulant, and avoid over-the-counter NSAIDs. Seek urgent help for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, severe headache, or bleeding that will not stop.
