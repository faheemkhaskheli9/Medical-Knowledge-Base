# Buspirone

**Class:** Anxiolytic (azapirone; serotonin 5-HT1A partial agonist). Not a benzodiazepine — see [diazepam](diazepam.md) for benzodiazepines.

## Dosing (adult, FDA, anxiety)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | Start 7.5 mg PO twice daily (15 mg/day); increase by 5 mg/day every 2–3 days as needed |
| Maximum | **60 mg per day** |
| With erythromycin, itraconazole or nefazodone | Low buspirone dose, e.g. 2.5 mg (labels differ: once daily or twice daily); adjust by clinical assessment |
| Paediatric, renal/hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

## Contraindications
- Other contraindications (e.g. hypersensitivity): see label.
- **MAOIs:** do not use buspirone with an MAOI intended to treat depression, or within 14 days of stopping either drug (serotonin syndrome and/or raised blood pressure). Starting buspirone in a patient treated with linezolid or IV methylene blue is also contraindicated.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- Serotonin syndrome with other serotonergic drugs — see label.
- Other warnings: see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| MAOIs, linezolid, IV methylene blue | Contraindicated (see above) |
| Erythromycin | Buspirone Cmax about 5-fold, AUC about 6-fold higher — use a low buspirone dose |
| Itraconazole | Cmax 13-fold, AUC 19-fold higher — use a low buspirone dose |
| Nefazodone | Cmax up to 20-fold, AUC up to 50-fold higher — use a low buspirone dose |
| Other CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g. ketoconazole, ritonavir) | May raise buspirone levels — see label |
| Rifampicin (rifampin) | Cmax about 84% and AUC about 90% lower — buspirone dose may need adjusting |
| Grapefruit juice (large amounts) | Cmax 4.3-fold, AUC 9.2-fold higher — avoid large amounts |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: serotonin syndrome (with serotonergic drugs) — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Response and adverse effects after dose changes; interacting medicines (especially new antibiotics, antifungals or antidepressants).

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take consistently as prescribed. Avoid drinking large amounts of grapefruit juice. Tell your prescriber and pharmacist about all your medicines, especially antidepressants (MAOIs), antibiotics such as erythromycin, antifungals and rifampicin. Seek urgent care for agitation, high temperature, sweating, shaking or muscle twitching.
