# Terbinafine (oral)

**Class:** Allylamine antifungal. Oral tablets only; topical products have separate labeling. Look-alike names: terbinafine (Lamisil) vs lamotrigine (Lamictal) — see [look-alike/sound-alike names](../look-alike-sound-alike.md) and [lamotrigine](lamotrigine.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA tablets, onychomycosis)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Fingernail onychomycosis | 250 mg PO once daily for 6 weeks |
| Toenail onychomycosis | 250 mg PO once daily for 12 weeks |
| Before starting | Evaluate for chronic or active liver disease; check liver function tests |
| Renal impairment (creatinine clearance 50 mL/min or less), hepatic cirrhosis | Terbinafine clearance reduced by about 50% — see label |
| Paediatric, other indications, older adults | See label |

## Contraindications
- Other contraindications (e.g. hypersensitivity): see label.
- **Chronic or active liver disease:** current generic labels list this as a contraindication; some Lamisil label versions say "not recommended" — see label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Hepatotoxicity:** liver failure, some leading to transplant or death, has occurred in people with and without pre-existing liver disease. Assess liver function before and periodically during treatment; stop if liver injury develops.
- **Taste and smell disturbance:** loss of taste or smell may be severe, prolonged or permanent — stop if it occurs.
- **Depressive symptoms:** reported; be alert for them and ask patients to report them.
- **Lupus erythematosus:** precipitation or exacerbation of cutaneous and systemic lupus reported.
- Serious skin and hypersensitivity reactions: see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| CYP2D6 substrates (e.g. desipramine) | Terbinafine inhibits CYP2D6 and increases desipramine levels — see label for other CYP2D6 substrates |
| Cimetidine, [fluconazole](fluconazole.md), ciclosporin, rifampicin, caffeine | Interactions reported — see label |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: liver failure, taste/smell loss, depressive symptoms, lupus, serious skin reactions.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Liver function before treatment and periodically during it; taste, smell and mood symptoms.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Treatment lasts weeks to months, and nails take longer still to look normal. Stop and contact your doctor promptly if you notice loss of taste or smell, low mood, or signs of liver problems such as yellow skin or eyes or dark urine. Tell your prescriber about all your medicines, including antidepressants.
