# Fluoxetine

**Class:** Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI). Potent CYP2D6 inhibitor with a long elimination half-life (label section "Long Elimination Half-Life" — see label). See also [sertraline](sertraline.md) and [citalopram](citalopram.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA, major depressive disorder)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult, MDD | Start 20 mg/day orally in the morning; doses above 20 mg/day may be given once daily in the morning or twice daily (morning and noon); maximum 80 mg/day |
| Other indications, paediatric | See label |
| Hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

## Contraindications
- **MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders:** contraindicated with fluoxetine or **within 5 weeks of stopping fluoxetine** (serotonin syndrome). Allow at least 14 days after stopping an MAOI before starting fluoxetine.
- **Pimozide and thioridazine:** contraindicated. Do not give thioridazine until at least 5 weeks after fluoxetine is stopped (serious ventricular arrhythmias and sudden death).
- Other contraindications: see label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Suicidal thoughts and behaviours** (antidepressant class boxed warning — see label for the exact wording and age groups); monitor for clinical worsening. See [crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md).
- **Serotonin syndrome:** the basis of the MAOI contraindication; other serotonergic combinations — see label.
- **Bleeding:** risk increased with NSAIDs, aspirin, warfarin or other drugs affecting coagulation.
- **Angle-closure glaucoma:** pupillary dilation may trigger an attack in people with anatomically narrow angles without a patent iridectomy.
- **Hyponatraemia (often SIADH):** sodium below 110 mmol/L reported; usually reversible on stopping. Higher risk in older adults and in patients on diuretics or who are volume depleted.
- **QT prolongation and ventricular arrhythmia, including torsades de pointes,** reported — caution with risk factors for QT prolongation.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| MAOIs | Contraindicated — 5-week washout after fluoxetine; 14 days after an MAOI |
| Pimozide, thioridazine | Contraindicated — raised levels via CYP2D6 inhibition; arrhythmia risk |
| CYP2D6 substrates | Fluoxetine can make a normal CYP2D6 metaboliser resemble a poor metaboliser — check doses of CYP2D6 substrates (see label) |
| NSAIDs ([ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md), [naproxen](naproxen.md)), [aspirin](aspirin.md), [warfarin](warfarin.md), other anticoagulants | Increased bleeding risk |
| Diuretics | Higher hyponatraemia risk |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) and [pharmacogenomics](../../pharmacogenomics/gene-drug-pairs.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: suicidal thoughts, serotonin syndrome, bleeding, hyponatraemia, angle-closure glaucoma, QT prolongation.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Mood and suicidal thoughts, especially early and after dose changes; sodium in older adults or those on diuretics; bleeding with antiplatelets/anticoagulants; ECG if QT risk factors.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take in the morning. Do not stop or change the dose without talking to your prescriber. Tell any prescriber or pharmacist you take fluoxetine — because it stays in the body for weeks, some medicines must wait about 5 weeks after the last dose. Seek urgent help for thoughts of self-harm, eye pain with blurred vision, or unusual bleeding.
