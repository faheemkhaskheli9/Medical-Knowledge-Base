# Levothyroxine

**Class / ATC:** Thyroid hormone — H03AA01. Narrow therapeutic index.

## Dosing (adult hypothyroidism)
| Patient | Starting dose |
|---------|---------------|
| Healthy adult < 60–65 years | Full replacement ~1.6 mcg/kg/day |
| Older adults | 25–50 mcg/day |
| Coronary artery disease | 12.5–25 mcg/day, increase slowly |

Adjust by 12.5–25 mcg; check **TSH 6–8 weeks** after any dose or brand change.

## How to take
On an empty stomach, 30–60 minutes before breakfast (or at bedtime ≥ 3 h after the last meal), with water, consistently. Separate by **4 hours** from calcium, iron, antacids, bile acid sequestrants. Keep the same product where possible.

## Pregnancy
Requirements rise early in pregnancy — on confirmation, increase dose (commonly ~20–30%, e.g. 2 extra doses per week) and check TSH every 4 weeks in the first half of pregnancy. Untreated hypothyroidism harms fetal development.

## Warnings
- Not for weight loss (US boxed warning) — toxic at high doses.
- Over-replacement: atrial fibrillation, bone loss (especially older adults).
- In adrenal insufficiency, start corticosteroids **before** levothyroxine.

## Interactions
PPIs and enzyme inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) may increase requirement; warfarin effect increases when thyroid status normalizes.
