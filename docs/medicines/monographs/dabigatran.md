# Dabigatran

**Class / ATC:** Direct oral anticoagulant, direct thrombin inhibitor — B01AE07 — **high-alert medication** ([high-alert list](../high-alert-medications.md)). Compare [apixaban](apixaban.md), [rivaroxaban](rivaroxaban.md) and [warfarin](warfarin.md).

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Non-valvular atrial fibrillation (FDA) | CrCl > 30 mL/min: 150 mg twice daily. CrCl 15–30 mL/min: 75 mg twice daily |
| Non-valvular atrial fibrillation (EU/UK SmPC) | 150 mg twice daily; 110 mg twice daily if aged 80 years or over or taking verapamil; individual assessment (thromboembolic vs bleeding risk) for other groups such as age 75–80 years |
| DVT/PE treatment and reduction of recurrence (FDA) | CrCl > 30 mL/min: 150 mg twice daily, after 5–10 days of parenteral anticoagulation |
| Prophylaxis after hip replacement (FDA) | CrCl > 30 mL/min: 110 mg 1–4 hours after surgery once hemostasis is achieved, then 220 mg once daily for 28–35 days (if not started on the day of surgery, start with 220 mg once daily) |

- **Renal:** dabigatran is mainly renally cleared, so renal function drives dosing. FDA: no dosing recommendation for DVT/PE treatment or prophylaxis at CrCl ≤ 30 mL/min or on dialysis, and none for AF at CrCl < 15 mL/min or on dialysis. **EU/UK SmPC: contraindicated at CrCl < 30 mL/min.** Use Cockcroft-Gault CrCl ([formulas](../../clinical-reference/formulas.md); [renal and hepatic dosing](../../special-populations/renal-hepatic.md)).
- **Capsules:** swallow whole with a full glass of water. Do **not** break, chew or empty the capsule — this increases exposure and bleeding risk.
- Children: weight-based pellets/capsules per label — specialist use.

## Contraindications
- Active pathological bleeding.
- History of serious hypersensitivity to dabigatran.
- **Mechanical prosthetic heart valve** (increased thrombosis and bleeding); use with bioprosthetic valves is not recommended.
- EU/UK SmPC also: severe renal impairment (CrCl < 30 mL/min); lesions or conditions with significant risk of major bleeding; use with certain strong P-gp inhibitors (see SmPC).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Premature discontinuation** increases the risk of thrombotic events (boxed warning) — if stopped for a reason other than pathological bleeding or completing a course, consider cover with another anticoagulant.
- **Spinal/epidural hematoma** with neuraxial anesthesia or spinal puncture, which may cause long-term or permanent paralysis (boxed warning).
- Bleeding risk rises with antiplatelets ([aspirin](aspirin.md), [clopidogrel](clopidogrel.md), [ticagrelor](ticagrelor.md)), NSAIDs ([ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md)), SSRIs/SNRIs and other anticoagulants.
- Triple-positive antiphospholipid syndrome: DOACs are not recommended.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| P-gp inducers (e.g. rifampicin/rifampin, St John's wort) | Lower dabigatran levels — avoid |
| Dronedarone or systemic ketoconazole (P-gp inhibitors) in AF with CrCl 30–50 mL/min | FDA: reduce to 75 mg twice daily |
| P-gp inhibitors with AF and CrCl < 30 mL/min | FDA: avoid co-administration |
| Verapamil | EU/UK: reduced dose (110 mg twice daily) in AF |
| Antiplatelets, NSAIDs, other anticoagulants | Additive bleeding risk |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: dyspepsia and gastritis-like symptoms, bleeding (bruising, nosebleeds, gum or menstrual bleeding).
- Serious: major or fatal bleeding (gastrointestinal, intracranial), spinal/epidural hematoma, hypersensitivity.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Limited human data; LMWH ([enoxaparin](enoxaparin.md)) is usually preferred in pregnancy. Check the current label and seek specialist advice. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Renal function before starting and regularly during treatment (more often in older adults, CKD, or acute illness/dehydration), hemoglobin, adherence, bleeding. Routine coagulation monitoring is not needed; INR does not reflect dabigatran effect.

## Overdose / antidote
Specific reversal agent: **idarucizumab** for emergency surgery/urgent procedures or life-threatening bleeding ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)). Unlike factor Xa inhibitors, dabigatran is partly removable by hemodialysis. Supportive care; stop the drug.

## Patient counselling points
Swallow capsules whole — never open them or sprinkle the contents. Keep them in the original pack (moisture protection). Do not stop without talking to your prescriber — stopping suddenly raises stroke and clot risk. Indigestion is common; report it rather than stopping. Tell every doctor, dentist and pharmacist you take an anticoagulant and avoid over-the-counter NSAIDs. Seek urgent help for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, severe headache, or bleeding that will not stop.
