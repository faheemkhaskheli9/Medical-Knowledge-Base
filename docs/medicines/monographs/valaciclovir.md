# Valaciclovir (valacyclovir) and aciclovir

**Class / ATC:** Antiviral nucleoside analogue (HSV, VZV) — valaciclovir J05AB11, aciclovir J05AB01. Valaciclovir is an oral prodrug of aciclovir with higher bioavailability, so it is taken less often. See [drug names by region](../drug-names-by-region.md).

## Dosing (valaciclovir, adult, normal renal function, FDA)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Cold sores (herpes labialis), age 12 years and over | 2 g twice daily for 1 day, doses about 12 hours apart, started at the first symptom |
| Genital herpes, initial episode | 1 g twice daily for 10 days (benefit not established if started more than 72 hours after onset) |
| Genital herpes, recurrent episode | 500 mg twice daily for 3 days (benefit not established if started more than 24 hours after onset) |
| Genital herpes, suppression (immunocompetent) | 1 g once daily; 500 mg once daily is an alternative for 9 or fewer recurrences a year |
| Herpes zoster (shingles) | 1 g three times daily for 7 days, started as early as possible (ideally within 48 hours of rash onset) |

### Renal impairment — herpes zoster example (FDA)
| CrCl (mL/min) | Valaciclovir dose |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 50 or more | 1 g every 8 hours |
| 30–49 | 1 g every 12 hours |
| 10–29 | 1 g every 24 hours |
| Less than 10 | 500 mg every 24 hours |

- Other indications have their own renal table in the label — always check it. Hemodialysis: give the dose after the session (about one third of body aciclovir is removed by a 4-hour session).
- Aciclovir (UK SmPC) is taken five times a day for herpes simplex and herpes zoster; it also needs renal dose adjustment. IV aciclovir dosing (encephalitis, immunocompromised) is weight-based and specialist — use the SmPC and local guidance.
- See [renal and hepatic dosing](../../special-populations/renal-hepatic.md).

## Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity (e.g. anaphylaxis) to valaciclovir, aciclovir or excipients.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Acute kidney injury:** reported, including in older adults and with dehydration or nephrotoxic drugs. Keep well hydrated; reduce the dose in renal impairment.
- **Neurotoxicity:** agitation, hallucinations, confusion, delirium, seizures and encephalopathy — mostly with doses too high for renal function and in older adults. Stop if they occur.
- **TTP/HUS:** fatal cases in advanced HIV and in transplant recipients in trials using 8 g/day. Stop at the first signs.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Nephrotoxic drugs (e.g. aminoglycosides, ciclosporin, NSAIDs such as [ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md)) | Higher risk of renal impairment — monitor renal function, hydrate |
| Drugs competing for renal tubular secretion (e.g. probenecid, cimetidine) | Higher aciclovir levels — relevant at high doses |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: headache, nausea, abdominal pain.
- Serious: acute kidney injury, neurotoxicity (confusion, hallucinations, seizures), TTP/HUS, hypersensitivity.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Aciclovir and valaciclovir are widely used in pregnancy for genital herpes under specialist advice; aciclovir passes into breast milk. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Renal function before treatment in older adults and at-risk patients; hydration; mental status (especially older adults and CKD).

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote. Overdose (often from unadjusted doses in renal impairment) can cause acute kidney injury and neurotoxicity; supportive care, and hemodialysis can remove aciclovir ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Start at the first tingle or sign of a cold sore, genital sore or shingles rash. Drink plenty of fluids. Tell your prescriber about kidney problems. Seek help if you become confused, see or hear things that are not there, or pass much less urine. Shingles near the eye, or any rash with severe headache, stiff neck or confusion, needs urgent medical review.
