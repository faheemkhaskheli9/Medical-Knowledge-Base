# Levetiracetam

**Class / ATC:** Antiepileptic — N03AX14

## Dosing (adult, oral or IV)
Start 500 mg twice daily; increase by 500 mg twice daily every 2 weeks as needed; max 1500 mg twice daily.

## Renal adjustment (renally cleared)
| CrCl (mL/min) | Dose range |
|---------------|------------|
| > 80 | 500–1500 mg every 12 h |
| 50–80 | 500–1000 mg every 12 h |
| 30–50 | 250–750 mg every 12 h |
| < 30 | 250–500 mg every 12 h |
| Dialysis | 500–1000 mg every 24 h + supplemental dose after dialysis |

## Adverse effects
Somnolence, dizziness, **irritability, aggression, mood changes, depression** (screen for suicidal thoughts — class warning for antiepileptics), rarely psychosis. Serious skin reactions rare.

## Advantages
Few drug interactions; no routine level monitoring.

## Pregnancy
Lamotrigine and levetiracetam have the lowest known risk of major malformations among commonly used antiepileptics (MHRA 2021). Do not stop antiepileptics suddenly — risk of seizures and SUDEP.
