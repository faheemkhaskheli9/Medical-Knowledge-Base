# Clopidogrel (P2Y12 inhibitors)

**Class / ATC:** P2Y12 inhibitor (antiplatelet), prodrug activated by CYP2C19 — B01AC04. Others: prasugrel, ticagrelor.

## Dosing (adult, oral)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| ACS / PCI | Loading 300–600 mg, then 75 mg once daily with aspirin (dual antiplatelet therapy, duration set by cardiology) |
| Stroke/TIA, peripheral arterial disease, aspirin intolerance | 75 mg once daily |

## Contraindications
Active pathological bleeding (e.g. peptic ulcer, intracranial hemorrhage); severe hepatic impairment.

## Warnings
- **CYP2C19 poor metabolizers** (two loss-of-function alleles, e.g. `*2`, `*3`) form little active drug — higher risk of stent thrombosis and cardiovascular events (FDA boxed warning). CPIC: use prasugrel or ticagrelor in poor and intermediate metabolizers after ACS/PCI if no contraindication. See [pharmacogenomics](../../pharmacogenomics/gene-drug-pairs.md).
- Bleeding; rare thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP).
- **Do not stop early after a coronary stent** without the cardiologist — stent thrombosis risk. For elective surgery the label advises stopping 5 days before if antiplatelet effect is not needed.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Omeprazole, esomeprazole | Reduce activation (CYP2C19 inhibition) — use pantoprazole if a PPI is needed |
| Other antiplatelets, anticoagulants, NSAIDs, SSRIs | Increased bleeding |
| Fluconazole, fluvoxamine, fluoxetine (strong/moderate CYP2C19 inhibitors) | Reduced effect — avoid |
| Repaglinide | Raised repaglinide levels (CYP2C8) — avoid |

## Adverse effects
Bleeding, bruising, GI upset, diarrhea, rash.

## Patient counselling
Do not stop without speaking to your cardiologist; tell dentists and surgeons you take it. Report black stools, blood in urine, or unusual bleeding.
