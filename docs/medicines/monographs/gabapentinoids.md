# Gabapentin and pregabalin (gabapentinoids)

**Class / ATC:** α2δ calcium-channel ligands — gabapentin N03AX12, pregabalin N03AX16. Controlled drugs in the UK (Schedule 3) and pregabalin is Schedule V in the US.

## Dosing (adult, oral, normal renal function)
| Drug | Start | Usual / max |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Gabapentin (neuropathic pain) | 300 mg on day 1, 300 mg twice daily day 2, 300 mg three times daily day 3 | Titrate to 900–3600 mg/day in 3 divided doses |
| Pregabalin (neuropathic pain, generalized anxiety) | 150 mg/day in 2–3 divided doses | Increase after 3–7 days; max 600 mg/day |

**Renally cleared — reduce dose when CrCl < 60 mL/min** (check label table). Older adults: start lower.

## Warnings
- **Respiratory depression** with opioids, benzodiazepines or other CNS depressants, in older adults, and in COPD (FDA 2019). Opioid + gabapentinoid increases opioid-overdose death risk.
- **Misuse and dependence** — ask about substance use; watch for early repeat requests.
- Suicidal thoughts (antiepileptic class warning).
- **Stop gradually** over at least 1 week (withdrawal: insomnia, anxiety, sweating; seizures in epilepsy).

## Adverse effects
Dizziness, somnolence, ataxia, blurred vision, weight gain, peripheral edema; rarely angioedema (pregabalin) and severe skin reactions.

## Interactions
Opioids, benzodiazepines, alcohol, sedating antihistamines: additive sedation and respiratory depression. Antacids with aluminium/magnesium reduce gabapentin absorption — take gabapentin 2 h after.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Pregabalin: possible small increase in major malformations (MHRA 2022) — use effective contraception and avoid unless clearly needed. Gabapentin: limited data; specialist advice.

## Patient counselling
Do not drive until you know how it affects you. Avoid alcohol. Do not share — it is a controlled medicine. Do not stop suddenly.
