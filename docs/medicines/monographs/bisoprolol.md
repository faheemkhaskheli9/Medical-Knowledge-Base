# Bisoprolol (beta-blockers)

**Class / ATC:** Cardioselective (β1) beta-blocker — C07AB07. Others: metoprolol, atenolol, carvedilol (non-selective + α), propranolol (non-selective).

## Dosing (adult, oral, once daily)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Heart failure with reduced EF (stable) | Start 1.25 mg; double every 1–2 weeks as tolerated to target 10 mg |
| Angina, AF rate control | 5–10 mg; max 20 mg |
| Hypertension | 5–10 mg; max 20 mg. Not first-line alone without another indication (NICE) |

## Contraindications
Acute or decompensated heart failure needing IV inotropes; cardiogenic shock; second- or third-degree AV block or sick sinus syndrome without a pacemaker; symptomatic bradycardia or hypotension; severe asthma; severe peripheral arterial disease or Raynaud's.

## Warnings
- **Do not stop abruptly** — rebound angina, MI, arrhythmia. Taper over 1–2 weeks.
- Asthma/COPD: cardioselective agents are usually tolerated in COPD; avoid in asthma unless a specialist agrees.
- Masks tachycardia and tremor of hypoglycemia (diabetes) and thyrotoxicosis.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Verapamil, diltiazem | Bradycardia, heart block, heart failure — avoid combination (especially IV verapamil) |
| Amiodarone, digoxin, ivabradine | Additive bradycardia |
| Insulin, sulfonylureas | Masked hypoglycemia |
| Clonidine | Rebound hypertension if clonidine is stopped first |

## Adverse effects
Fatigue, cold extremities, bradycardia, dizziness, sleep disturbance, bronchospasm, erectile dysfunction; transient worsening of heart failure during titration.

## Monitoring
Heart rate, blood pressure, symptoms and signs of congestion at each titration step.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Labetalol is the preferred beta-blocker for hypertension in pregnancy; beta-blockers may cause fetal growth restriction and neonatal bradycardia/hypoglycemia — specialist advice.
