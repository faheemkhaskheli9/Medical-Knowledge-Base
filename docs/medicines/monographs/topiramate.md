# Topiramate

**Class / ATC:** Antiepileptic (sulfamate-substituted monosaccharide; weak carbonic anhydrase inhibitor) — N03AX11. Also used for migraine prevention. Compare [valproate](valproate.md), [lamotrigine](lamotrigine.md), [levetiracetam](levetiracetam.md).

## Dosing (FDA)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Epilepsy monotherapy, adults and children ≥ 10 years | Start 25 mg twice daily (week 1) and titrate weekly per label schedule to the recommended **400 mg/day in two divided doses** |
| Migraine prevention, ≥ 12 years | Start 25 mg in the evening (week 1), increase weekly per label schedule to **100 mg/day in two divided doses** |
| Renal impairment (CrCl < 70 mL/min/1.73 m²) | **One-half** of the usual adult dose |
| Hemodialysis | Rapidly removed — a supplemental dose may be needed; adjust for dialysis duration and system clearance |

- Titrate slowly to reduce cognitive adverse effects; withdraw gradually (abrupt withdrawal can provoke seizures).
- Adjunctive epilepsy and pediatric dosing: see label.

## Contraindications
- **UK/EU:** pregnancy, and women of childbearing potential not meeting the **Pregnancy Prevention Programme** conditions; for **migraine prevention, contraindicated in pregnancy**; for epilepsy, not in pregnancy unless no other suitable treatment.
- Hypersensitivity to topiramate.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Fetal toxicity:** increased risk of major congenital malformations, including oral clefts (cleft lip and/or palate), and of small-for-gestational-age infants (FDA). MHRA review also found possible increased risk of intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder and ADHD after in-utero exposure. UK/EU Pregnancy Prevention Programme: highly effective contraception, pregnancy test before starting, annual risk acknowledgement form. See [valproate](valproate.md) for the analogous programme.
- **Acute myopia and secondary angle-closure glaucoma** — stop and seek urgent ophthalmology review for sudden eye pain or blurred vision; untreated raised eye pressure can cause permanent vision loss.
- **Visual field defects.**
- **Metabolic acidosis** — measure serum bicarbonate at baseline and periodically; reduce or stop if clinically appropriate.
- **Kidney stones** — maintain adequate fluid intake; avoid with other carbonic anhydrase inhibitors or a ketogenic diet.
- **Oligohidrosis and hyperthermia**, especially in children and hot weather.
- **Hyperammonemia** with or without encephalopathy, with or without [valproate](valproate.md) (also hypothermia with valproate) — check ammonia if unexplained lethargy, vomiting or mental status change.
- **Cognitive and neuropsychiatric effects** (word-finding difficulty, slowed thinking, somnolence, depression, mood problems); **suicidal thoughts and behavior** (class warning for antiepileptics).

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Combined hormonal contraceptives | Reduced contraceptive efficacy and breakthrough bleeding, especially at doses > 200 mg/day — use a method not affected (UK PPP requires highly effective contraception) |
| [Valproate](valproate.md) | Hyperammonemia, encephalopathy, hypothermia |
| [Phenytoin](phenytoin.md), [carbamazepine](carbamazepine.md) | Lower topiramate levels; phenytoin levels may rise |
| Other carbonic anhydrase inhibitors (e.g. acetazolamide) | Metabolic acidosis, kidney stones — avoid |
| Alcohol, CNS depressants | Additive sedation |
| [Metformin](metformin.md) | Metabolic acidosis risk — monitor |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: paresthesia, fatigue, dizziness, somnolence, cognitive slowing, weight loss and reduced appetite, taste change.
- Serious: angle-closure glaucoma, metabolic acidosis, nephrolithiasis, hyperthermia, hyperammonemic encephalopathy, suicidality, serious skin reactions.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See contraindications and warnings above; specialist review for anyone pregnant or planning pregnancy — do not stop suddenly for epilepsy (seizure risk). See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Serum bicarbonate, renal function, eye symptoms, mood and cognition, weight; pregnancy status and contraception at every review (annual PPP review in UK).

## Overdose / antidote
CNS depression, convulsions, metabolic acidosis — supportive care; hemodialysis removes topiramate ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Drink plenty of fluids. Seek urgent help for sudden eye pain, redness or blurred vision. Tingling of fingers and toes is common. Topiramate can harm an unborn baby — if you could become pregnant, use highly effective contraception and talk to your doctor before planning pregnancy; do not stop it without advice if you have epilepsy. The pill may not work reliably with topiramate. Report low mood, thoughts of self-harm, or reduced sweating with overheating.
