# Semaglutide

**Class:** GLP-1 receptor agonist. Brands are product-specific: **Ozempic** (weekly injection, type 2 diabetes; also cardiovascular and kidney risk reduction per label), **Rybelsus** (daily tablet, type 2 diabetes), **Wegovy** (weekly injection, chronic weight management and cardiovascular risk reduction per label). See [type 2 diabetes](../../conditions/type-2-diabetes.md) and [SGLT2 inhibitors](sglt2-inhibitors.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA labels)
| Product | Dose |
|---------|------|
| **Ozempic** (SC once weekly, any time of day, with or without food) | **0.25 mg once weekly for 4 weeks** (initiation dose — not effective for glycaemic control), then **0.5 mg once weekly**; if needed after at least 4 weeks, 1 mg once weekly; then after at least 4 weeks, 2 mg once weekly. **Maximum 2 mg once weekly** |
| Ozempic missed dose | Give within 5 days of the missed dose; if more than 5 days, skip it and resume on the usual day |
| **Wegovy** (SC once weekly) | Escalate every 4 weeks: 0.25 mg → 0.5 mg → 1 mg → 1.7 mg → 2.4 mg; maintenance per label — see label for missed-dose and slower-titration rules |
| **Rybelsus** (oral, once daily) | Dose steps depend on the tablet formulation — see label. Take on an empty stomach on waking with a small amount of plain water, at least 30 minutes before food, drink or other oral medicines |
| Renal or hepatic impairment | No dose adjustment in the labels; monitor renal function if GI losses cause dehydration |
| Paediatric | Product-specific (Wegovy has paediatric dosing) — see label |

- Products are **not interchangeable** — do not substitute one brand for another, or a compounded product for an approved one, without a new dosing plan. FDA has received reports of dosing errors (including mL/unit confusion) with compounded semaglutide.
- Do not combine with another GLP-1 receptor agonist.

## Contraindications
- **Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or multiple endocrine neoplasia type 2 (MEN 2).**
- Serious hypersensitivity to semaglutide or any excipient.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — thyroid C-cell tumours** in rodents; human relevance unknown. Counsel about symptoms of thyroid tumours (neck lump, dysphagia, persistent hoarseness).
- **Acute pancreatitis:** stop if suspected; do not restart if confirmed.
- **Diabetic retinopathy complications** (Ozempic, Rybelsus): rapid improvement in glucose control can worsen retinopathy — monitor patients with retinopathy.
- **Hypoglycaemia** with insulin or a sulfonylurea — consider reducing their dose (see [insulin](insulin.md), [gliclazide](gliclazide.md)).
- **Acute kidney injury** from dehydration due to nausea, vomiting or diarrhoea.
- Gallbladder disease (cholelithiasis, cholecystitis); hypersensitivity reactions; increased heart rate.
- **Pulmonary aspiration** during general anaesthesia or deep sedation (delayed gastric emptying) — tell the anaesthetist.
- Wegovy: suicidal behaviour and ideation — monitor.
- **NAION (non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy):** EMA concluded in 2025 that it is a very rare side effect (up to 1 in 10,000); stop semaglutide if NAION is confirmed. Sudden vision loss needs urgent assessment.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| [Insulin](insulin.md), sulfonylureas ([gliclazide](gliclazide.md)) | Hypoglycaemia — consider lowering their dose |
| Oral medicines | Delayed gastric emptying may change absorption of oral drugs — take care with narrow-therapeutic-index drugs; with Rybelsus, [levothyroxine](levothyroxine.md) exposure increased — monitor thyroid tests (see label) |
| Other GLP-1 receptor agonists | Do not combine |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, abdominal pain, constipation (mostly during dose escalation) — see label.
- Serious: pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, acute kidney injury, hypoglycaemia (with insulin/sulfonylureas), retinopathy complications, serious hypersensitivity, NAION (very rare) — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Stop **at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy** (long washout); weight-management products are not for use in pregnancy. See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
HbA1c or weight per indication; renal function if GI side effects; retinal screening in diabetic retinopathy; symptoms of pancreatitis or gallbladder disease; mood (Wegovy); hypoglycaemia with insulin/sulfonylureas.

## Overdose / antidote
Severe nausea, vomiting and hypoglycaemia possible; long half-life (about 1 week) means prolonged observation — supportive care; no specific antidote — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Inject once a week on the same day; your dose is increased slowly to reduce sickness. Eat smaller meals and stop eating when full. Drink enough fluids if you have vomiting or diarrhoea. Seek urgent care for severe stomach pain that may spread to the back, sudden loss of vision, or signs of a severe allergic reaction. Tell your doctor about a lump in the neck or trouble swallowing, and tell the anaesthetist before any procedure with sedation. Stop at least 2 months before trying for a baby. Use only the brand and pen you were prescribed.
