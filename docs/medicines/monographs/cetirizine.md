# Cetirizine (non-sedating antihistamines)

**Class / ATC:** Second-generation H1 antihistamine — R06AE07. Others: levocetirizine, loratadine, desloratadine, fexofenadine.

## Dosing (oral)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adults and children 12 years and older | 10 mg once daily |
| Children 6–11 years | US: 5–10 mg once daily (OTC label). UK: 5 mg twice daily (BNFc) |
| Children 2–5 years | US: 2.5 mg once daily, may increase to 5 mg once daily or 2.5 mg every 12 hours (OTC label). UK: 2.5 mg twice daily (BNFc) |
| Renal impairment (UK SmPC) | CrCl 30–49 mL/min: 5 mg once daily; CrCl 10–29 mL/min: 5 mg every 2 days; **CrCl < 10 mL/min: contraindicated** |
| Hepatic impairment with renal impairment | Adjust as for renal impairment |
| Older adults (65 years and older) | US OTC label: ask a doctor before use |

Max 10 mg in 24 hours in adults. Under 2 years: specialist advice only.

## Other agents (adult doses)
| Drug | Dose |
|------|------|
| Loratadine | 10 mg once daily |
| Fexofenadine | US: 180 mg once daily (or 60 mg twice daily); UK: 120 mg once daily for seasonal allergic rhinitis, 180 mg for urticaria. Fruit juices (grapefruit, orange, apple) reduce absorption — take with water |

## Indications
Allergic rhinitis and conjunctivitis, chronic urticaria, itch. **Not a treatment for anaphylaxis** — adrenaline (epinephrine) IM is first-line ([first aid](../../first-aid/first-aid.md), [red flags](../../guidelines/red-flags.md)).

## Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to cetirizine, levocetirizine or hydroxyzine. Severe renal impairment (CrCl < 10 mL/min, UK SmPC).

## Warnings
- Drowsiness in some people (more often than with loratadine or fexofenadine) — caution with driving and alcohol.
- Caution with urinary retention risk (prostatic hyperplasia, spinal cord lesions) and epilepsy.
- **Pruritus after stopping** (FDA 2025): rare, severe itching days after stopping long-term (months to years) daily use of cetirizine or levocetirizine. In many reported cases restarting the drug relieved it. Discuss before long-term daily use.

## Interactions
Few — minimal CYP metabolism. Additive sedation with alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines and other sedatives.

## Adverse effects
- Common: drowsiness, fatigue, headache, dry mouth.
- Serious (rare): hypersensitivity, urinary retention, pruritus after withdrawal (above).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Cetirizine and loratadine have the most reassuring pregnancy data among antihistamines and are usual choices when one is needed. Small amounts pass into breast milk; occasional standard doses are considered acceptable, but watch the infant for drowsiness or irritability with regular use (LactMed).

## Overdose
Drowsiness in adults; children may become agitated or restless before drowsiness. Supportive care. See [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling
Take once a day; do not exceed the stated dose. Avoid alcohol and do not drive if drowsy. Check combination cold and allergy products for a second antihistamine. Get emergency help for swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, wheeze or trouble breathing — antihistamine tablets are not enough. Ask a pharmacist before using daily for many months.
