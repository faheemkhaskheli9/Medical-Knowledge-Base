# Clonidine

**Class:** Centrally acting alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. Used for hypertension (not first-line — see [hypertension](../../conditions/hypertension.md)); separate products are licensed for ADHD (extended-release), menopausal flushing and migraine prophylaxis in some countries, and specialist epidural analgesia.

## Dosing (adult, FDA Catapres immediate-release tablets, hypertension)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | Start **0.1 mg (100 micrograms) PO twice daily** (morning and bedtime); increase by 0.1 mg/day at weekly intervals if needed |
| Usual range | 0.2 mg to 0.6 mg per day in divided doses |
| Renal impairment | Adjust to the degree of impairment and monitor carefully — see label |
| Older adults | Lower starting dose may be needed — see label |
| Transdermal patch, extended-release tablets (ADHD) | Different products and doses — **not interchangeable** with tablets mg-for-mg; see each label |
| Paediatric | See label |

- **Unit error risk:** US tablets are labelled in mg (0.1 mg, 0.2 mg, 0.3 mg); UK/other products in micrograms (e.g. 25 micrograms, 100 micrograms). 0.1 mg = 100 micrograms. Write "micrograms" in full — see [unsafe abbreviations](../../clinical-reference/unsafe-abbreviations.md).

## Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to clonidine (transdermal: including to adhesive components).
- Some non-US labels also list severe bradyarrhythmia (sick sinus syndrome, second- or third-degree AV block) — see local label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Do not stop abruptly:** rebound hypertension with nervousness, agitation, headache, tremor; rarely hypertensive encephalopathy, stroke or death. **Taper over 2 to 4 days** when stopping tablets. Risk is higher with higher doses and with a concomitant beta-blocker.
- **With a beta-blocker:** if both are to be stopped, withdraw the beta-blocker **several days before** gradually stopping clonidine.
- Bradycardia and AV block, especially with other drugs that slow sinus node or AV conduction.
- Sedation and impaired alertness; additive with alcohol and other CNS depressants.
- Continue through surgery where possible (give up to 4 hours before and resume soon after) — see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Beta-blockers ([propranolol](propranolol.md), [bisoprolol](bisoprolol.md)) | Worse rebound hypertension on clonidine withdrawal; additive bradycardia — stop the beta-blocker first |
| [Digoxin](digoxin.md), [diltiazem](diltiazem.md), verapamil | Bradycardia and AV block reported |
| Tricyclic antidepressants ([amitriptyline](amitriptyline.md)) | May reduce clonidine's antihypertensive effect |
| Alcohol, benzodiazepines, [opioids](opioids.md), other sedatives | Additive CNS depression |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: dry mouth, drowsiness, dizziness, constipation, sedation — see label; patch: local skin reactions.
- Serious: rebound hypertension on withdrawal, bradycardia, AV block, hypotension — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Blood pressure and heart rate (including postural BP); sedation; adherence (missed doses can cause rebound); taper plan when stopping.

## Overdose / antidote
Hypotension or early hypertension, bradycardia, respiratory depression, miosis, drowsiness and coma; **children are vulnerable to small amounts, including chewed or swallowed patches**. Emergency care; supportive treatment — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Never stop clonidine suddenly or run out — your blood pressure can rise sharply; your prescriber will reduce the dose over several days. It can make you drowsy and give you a dry mouth; avoid alcohol and take care driving. Keep tablets and used patches away from children — fold used patches in half and dispose of them safely. Seek urgent care for a severe headache, confusion, chest pain or a very slow heartbeat.
