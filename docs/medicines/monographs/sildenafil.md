# Sildenafil (PDE5 inhibitors)

**Class / ATC:** Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor — G04BE03. Others: tadalafil, vardenafil, avanafil. Indications: erectile dysfunction (Viagra) and pulmonary arterial hypertension (Revatio — a separate product and regimen).

## Dosing (adult, erectile dysfunction, FDA)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Most adults | 50 mg as needed about 1 hour before sexual activity (range 30 minutes to 4 hours); maximum once daily; may increase to a maximum of 100 mg or decrease to 25 mg |
| Age over 65 years (FDA) | Consider starting 25 mg (EU SmPC: no adjustment needed in older adults) |
| Hepatic impairment (e.g. cirrhosis), severe renal impairment (CrCl below 30 mL/min) | Consider starting 25 mg |
| On a stable alpha-blocker | Start 25 mg |
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, saquinavir) or erythromycin | Consider starting 25 mg |
| Ritonavir | Do not exceed a single 25 mg dose in 48 hours |

- Pulmonary arterial hypertension (Revatio): 20 mg three times daily orally — specialist only; do not use Viagra and Revatio together.

## Contraindications
- **Nitrates or nitric oxide donors in any form** (regular or intermittent, e.g. glyceryl trinitrate spray, isosorbide mononitrate, amyl nitrite "poppers") — severe hypotension. It is not known when nitrates can safely be given after a dose.
- Guanylate cyclase stimulators (riociguat).
- Hypersensitivity.
- EU SmPC also lists: men for whom sexual activity is inadvisable (e.g. severe cardiovascular disease), severe hepatic impairment, hypotension, recent stroke or MI, known hereditary retinal degeneration, and previous NAION.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Cardiovascular risk of sexual activity:** assess cardiovascular status before treatment.
- **Sudden vision loss (NAION):** stop and seek urgent care for sudden loss of vision in one or both eyes.
- **Sudden hearing loss** with or without tinnitus/dizziness — stop and seek care.
- **Priapism:** erection lasting more than 4 hours needs emergency treatment.
- **Hypotension** with alpha-blockers ([tamsulosin](tamsulosin.md), doxazosin) and antihypertensives; caution in left ventricular outflow obstruction.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Nitrates (any form), riociguat | Profound hypotension — **contraindicated** |
| Alpha-blockers | Symptomatic hypotension — be stable on the alpha-blocker first; start 25 mg |
| Ritonavir and other strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, [clarithromycin](clarithromycin.md), erythromycin | Large rise in sildenafil exposure — dose limits above |
| Alcohol, antihypertensives ([amlodipine](amlodipine.md) etc.) | Additive blood pressure lowering |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: headache, flushing, dyspepsia, nasal congestion, visual disturbance (blue tinge, light sensitivity), back pain.
- Serious: hypotension, MI or arrhythmia, NAION, sudden hearing loss, priapism.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Not indicated for women in the erectile dysfunction label. PAH use in pregnancy is specialist. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Cardiovascular assessment, blood pressure, interacting drugs (nitrates, alpha-blockers, HIV protease inhibitors), visual or hearing symptoms.

## Overdose / antidote
Exaggerated adverse effects (hypotension, headache, flushing). No specific antidote; supportive care; not removed by dialysis ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Never take with nitrate medicines (including GTN spray for angina) or "poppers". Tell any paramedic or emergency doctor that you have taken it, especially if you have chest pain. Take no more than one dose a day. Seek urgent help for an erection lasting more than 4 hours, sudden loss of vision or hearing, or chest pain during sex. Buy only from legitimate pharmacies — illicit products may contain unknown doses.
