# Metronidazole

**Class / ATC:** Nitroimidazole antibacterial and antiprotozoal — J01XD01 (IV), P01AB01 (oral). Active against anaerobes and protozoa (Trichomonas, Giardia, Entamoeba).

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Bacterial vaginosis | 500 mg PO twice daily for 7 days (CDC 2021) |
| Trichomoniasis | Women: 500 mg PO twice daily for 7 days; men: 2 g PO single dose (CDC 2021) |
| Anaerobic infections (UK) | 400 mg PO every 8 hours (BNF); IV 500 mg every 8 hours |
| Anaerobic infections (US) | 7.5 mg/kg PO or IV every 6 hours; max 4 g in 24 hours (FDA) |
| Amebiasis (intestinal) | 750 mg PO three times daily for 5–10 days (FDA) |
| Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) | Reduce dose by 50% (FDA) |
| Renal impairment | No routine adjustment; removed by hemodialysis — give the dose after dialysis |

- *C. difficile*: no longer first-line — oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin preferred; metronidazole only for a non-severe initial episode when those are unavailable (IDSA/SHEA 2021).
- Also used in combination regimens for *H. pylori* and for surgical prophylaxis — follow local protocols ([antimicrobial stewardship](../../infections/antimicrobial-stewardship.md)).

## Contraindications
- **Disulfiram** within the past 2 weeks (psychotic reactions).
- **Alcohol** or products containing propylene glycol during treatment and for at least 3 days after (FDA; BNF advises at least 48 hours) — disulfiram-like reaction.
- **Cockayne syndrome** — severe, sometimes fatal hepatotoxicity.
- Hypersensitivity to metronidazole or other nitroimidazoles.

## Warnings
- **Boxed warning (FDA):** carcinogenic in mice and rats — avoid unnecessary use.
- **Neurotoxicity:** peripheral neuropathy (mainly with prolonged courses), encephalopathy, seizures, aseptic meningitis — stop if neurological symptoms develop.
- Severe skin reactions (SJS/TEN) reported.
- QT prolongation reported, mainly with other QT-prolonging drugs.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Warfarin** | Raised INR, bleeding — monitor INR closely, often needs dose reduction ([warfarin](warfarin.md), [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md)) |
| Alcohol, disulfiram | Disulfiram-like reaction (flushing, vomiting, tachycardia); psychotic reactions with disulfiram |
| **Lithium** | Raised lithium levels — monitor ([lithium](lithium.md)) |
| Busulfan | Raised busulfan levels and toxicity |
| Phenytoin, phenobarbital (inducers) | Reduced metronidazole levels |

## Adverse effects
- Common: nausea, metallic taste, abdominal discomfort, headache, dark urine (harmless).
- Serious: peripheral and central neurotoxicity, severe skin reactions, leucopenia with long courses.

## Pregnancy / lactation
CDC 2021 states metronidazole can be used in all trimesters for bacterial vaginosis and trichomoniasis. Present in breast milk; after a 2 g single dose, CDC suggests withholding breastfeeding for 12–24 hours.

## Patient counselling
Do not drink alcohol during the course and for at least 2–3 days after the last dose. Take with or after food to reduce nausea. A metallic taste and darker urine are common. Report numbness, tingling, unsteadiness, confusion or a rash. Complete the prescribed course.
