# Simvastatin

**Class:** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (statin). See also [atorvastatin](atorvastatin.md) and [rosuvastatin](rosuvastatin.md) for general statin points.

## Dosing (adult, FDA Zocor label)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | Once daily in the evening. Usual starting dose and range depend on indication and label version — see label |
| 80 mg dose | **Restricted** to patients who have taken simvastatin 80 mg daily chronically (e.g. 12 months or more) without evidence of muscle toxicity; higher myopathy risk than lower doses |
| With verapamil, diltiazem or dronedarone | Do not exceed 10 mg daily |
| With amiodarone, amlodipine or ranolazine | Do not exceed 20 mg daily |
| With lomitapide | Reduce the simvastatin dose by 50%; do not exceed 20 mg once daily (40 mg once daily in patients previously on 80 mg chronically) |
| Severe renal impairment | Start 5 mg daily with close monitoring (some labels define severe as creatinine clearance 15–29 mL/min) |
| Paediatric, hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

- Older labels used different interaction caps (e.g. 2010 label) — use the current label. UK limits differ: see the SmPC and [diltiazem](diltiazem.md).
- Some strengths (5 mg, 80 mg) are not marketed in every country or for every brand.

## Contraindications
- **Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors** (e.g. select azole antifungals, macrolide antibiotics, some antivirals, nefazodone). If short-term treatment with a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor is unavoidable, suspend simvastatin for the duration of that treatment.
- **Gemfibrozil, ciclosporin (cyclosporine) or danazol.**
- Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis.
- Other contraindications (hypersensitivity; pregnancy and breastfeeding wording varies by label version): see label.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Myopathy and rhabdomyolysis:** risk rises with dose (highest at 80 mg) and with drugs that raise simvastatin levels — e.g. erythromycin, clarithromycin, telithromycin, HIV protease inhibitors, nefazodone — and with niacin, fibrates, colchicine and grapefruit juice.
- Increased myopathy risk was reported in Chinese patients taking simvastatin 40 mg with lipid-modifying doses of niacin — see label for the current recommendation.
- Liver: see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors ([clarithromycin](clarithromycin.md), itraconazole, ketoconazole, HIV protease inhibitors, nefazodone) | Contraindicated — suspend simvastatin during short courses |
| Gemfibrozil, ciclosporin, danazol | Contraindicated |
| Verapamil, [diltiazem](diltiazem.md), dronedarone | Maximum 10 mg daily |
| [Amiodarone](amiodarone.md), [amlodipine](amlodipine.md), ranolazine | Maximum 20 mg daily |
| Lomitapide | Halve the dose; maximum 20 mg daily (see table) |
| Grapefruit juice | Avoid (current label) |
| Niacin, other fibrates, [colchicine](colchicine.md) | Increased myopathy risk — see label |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: myopathy, rhabdomyolysis; liver injury — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label (wording changed in recent label revisions) and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Lipids per guideline; muscle symptoms, especially after dose increases or new interacting drugs; liver tests as the label directs; renal function.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take once daily in the evening. Avoid grapefruit juice. Tell every prescriber and pharmacist you take simvastatin — some antibiotics (e.g. clarithromycin, erythromycin), antifungals and heart medicines must not be combined with it or need a lower simvastatin dose. Report unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness, especially with fever or dark urine.
