# Carbimazole (thionamides)

**Class / ATC:** Thionamide antithyroid drug — H03BB01. Prodrug of **thiamazole (methimazole)**. Alternative: propylthiouracil (PTU). Inhibits thyroid hormone synthesis. Used for hyperthyroidism (Graves' disease, toxic nodular goitre) and to prepare for thyroidectomy or radioiodine. See [levothyroxine](levothyroxine.md) for "block and replace".

## Dosing (oral, adult, UK SmPC)
| Phase | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Initial | 20–60 mg daily in 2–3 divided doses, titrated against thyroid function until euthyroid |
| Maintenance (titration regimen) | Usually 5–15 mg daily |
| Block and replace | Higher carbimazole dose with levothyroxine — specialist regimen |

- Specialist (endocrinology) initiation is usual. BNF doses differ slightly — check the BNF. Children: weight- and age-based (BNFc).
- Beta-blockers (e.g. propranolol) are often used for symptom control until euthyroid ([bisoprolol](bisoprolol.md) page covers beta-blocker cautions).

## Contraindications
- Hypersensitivity to carbimazole or thiamazole.
- Serious pre-existing blood disorders; severe hepatic insufficiency.
- **History of acute pancreatitis** after carbimazole or thiamazole (MHRA 2019, 2025).

## Warnings
- **Agranulocytosis / neutropenia** (bone marrow suppression) — rare but can be fatal. Tell every patient to **stop the drug and seek medical advice immediately** for sore throat, mouth ulcers, fever, bruising or bleeding, or malaise; check a full blood count urgently and stop if neutropenia is confirmed.
- **Acute pancreatitis:** stop immediately and permanently; do not re-expose.
- **Congenital malformations:** increased risk, especially with first-trimester exposure and high doses (15 mg daily or more) — women of childbearing potential should use **effective contraception** (MHRA 2019).
- Hepatotoxicity (cholestatic jaundice, hepatitis) — stop if it occurs.
- Over-treatment causes hypothyroidism — monitor thyroid function.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| [Warfarin](warfarin.md) | Anticoagulant effect may change as thyroid status changes — monitor INR |
| [Digoxin](digoxin.md), theophylline, [bisoprolol](bisoprolol.md) and other beta-blockers | Dose needs may change as the patient becomes euthyroid |
| Other drugs that cause bone marrow suppression (e.g. clozapine — [antipsychotics](antipsychotics.md)) | Additive agranulocytosis risk |

## Adverse effects
- Common: rash, itching, nausea, headache, joint pain, taste disturbance, mild hair loss.
- Serious: agranulocytosis, aplastic anemia, acute pancreatitis, hepatitis or cholestatic jaundice, vasculitis (rare).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Use in pregnancy only after an individual benefit-risk assessment, at the **lowest effective dose** and without added thyroid hormone (no block-and-replace), with close maternal, fetal and neonatal monitoring. Untreated hyperthyroidism is itself harmful — do not simply stop. PTU is often preferred in the **first trimester** (lower malformation risk) and carbimazole/methimazole later (PTU carries a boxed warning for severe liver injury). Specialist care; see [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md). Breastfeeding: present in breast milk — use the lowest dose with specialist advice and infant monitoring.

## Monitoring
Thyroid function (free T4, T3, TSH) regularly during titration per specialist protocol, then less often; full blood count if any symptoms of infection; liver function if symptoms; pregnancy status and contraception.

## Overdose / antidote
Limited data; possible hypothyroidism and bone marrow suppression. Supportive care; contact a poison center ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
**Stop the tablets and get an urgent blood test** if you develop a sore throat, mouth ulcers, fever, bruising or feel generally unwell. Seek urgent help for severe abdominal pain (possible pancreatitis) or yellowing of the skin. Use reliable contraception and tell your doctor before trying for a baby or as soon as you know you are pregnant. Keep thyroid blood test appointments.
