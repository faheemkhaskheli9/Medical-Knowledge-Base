# Amoxicillin (± clavulanate)

**Class / ATC:** Aminopenicillin — J01CA04; with clavulanic acid (co-amoxiclav) — J01CR02

**Mechanism:** Inhibits bacterial cell-wall synthesis; clavulanate inhibits beta-lactamases.

## Dosing
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult (amoxicillin) | 500 mg PO every 8 h, or 875 mg every 12 h (US); up to 1 g every 8 h for severe infection/CAP |
| Adult (co-amoxiclav) | 500/125 mg every 8 h or 875/125 mg every 12 h |
| Children (amoxicillin) | 25–50 mg/kg/day divided every 8–12 h; **high dose 80–90 mg/kg/day** divided every 12 h (e.g. otitis media) |
| CrCl 10–30 mL/min | 250–500 mg every 12 h; avoid 875 mg tablets |
| CrCl < 10 mL/min | 250–500 mg every 24 h |

Do not exceed the adult dose in children. Co-amoxiclav pediatric doses depend on the formulation ratio — check the product.

## Contraindications
- Serious hypersensitivity to any beta-lactam ([cross-reactivity](../../allergy/cross-reactivity.md)).
- Co-amoxiclav: previous cholestatic jaundice/hepatic dysfunction with co-amoxiclav.

## Warnings
- Non-allergic maculopapular rash is common in **infectious mononucleosis (EBV)** — avoid in suspected glandular fever.
- Co-amoxiclav: cholestatic hepatitis (may appear after stopping), more diarrhea.
- *C. difficile*-associated diarrhea.

## Interactions
Methotrexate (reduced clearance), warfarin (INR may rise — monitor), allopurinol (more rash).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Generally considered safe in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
