# Tizanidine

**Class:** Centrally acting alpha-2 adrenergic agonist; muscle relaxant for spasticity. See also [baclofen](baclofen.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA Zanaflex)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | Start 2 mg PO; may repeat every 6–8 hours as needed, up to 3 doses in 24 hours; increase gradually |
| Maximum | **36 mg per day**; single doses above 16 mg have not been studied |
| Renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 25 mL/min) | Clearance reduced by more than 50% — use caution and lower individual doses during titration; if higher doses are needed, increase the individual dose rather than the frequency |
| With a moderate or weak CYP1A2 inhibitor (if clinically necessary) | Start 2 mg and increase in 2–4 mg steps daily based on response |
| Hepatic impairment, older adults, tablets vs capsules (fed/fasted differences) | See label |

## Contraindications
- **Potent CYP1A2 inhibitors, such as fluvoxamine or [ciprofloxacin](ciprofloxacin.md).**
- Hypersensitivity to tizanidine.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Hypotension:** alpha-2 agonist; clinically significant hypotension reported with fluvoxamine or ciprofloxacin and single 4 mg doses. Monitor with antihypertensives.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** liver function abnormalities and hepatotoxicity observed — monitor aminotransferases.
- **Withdrawal:** abrupt stopping can cause rebound hypertension, tachycardia and hypertonia. Reduce the dose slowly (2–4 mg per day), especially after high doses or long-term use.
- Sedation: see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Fluvoxamine, [ciprofloxacin](ciprofloxacin.md) (potent CYP1A2 inhibitors) | Contraindicated — large rise in tizanidine levels, hypotension, sedation |
| Moderate or weak CYP1A2 inhibitors | If clinically necessary, start 2 mg and increase in 2–4 mg steps daily (see label) |
| Oral contraceptives | May reduce tizanidine clearance by up to 50% — current label: concomitant use not recommended (older labels: reduce starting dose and titration rate) |
| Antihypertensives | Additive hypotension — monitor |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: hypotension, hepatotoxicity, withdrawal rebound hypertension.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Blood pressure; liver aminotransferases; sedation; interacting medicines (especially new antibiotics or antidepressants).

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Do not stop suddenly — your prescriber will reduce the dose slowly. Tell every prescriber and pharmacist you take tizanidine: ciprofloxacin and fluvoxamine must not be taken with it, and hormonal contraceptives can raise its level. Get up slowly to avoid dizziness or fainting. Seek medical advice for yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or unusual tiredness.
