# Diltiazem (rate-limiting calcium-channel blockers)

**Class / ATC:** Non-dihydropyridine (benzothiazepine) calcium-channel blocker — C08DB01. Slows heart rate and AV conduction, unlike dihydropyridines such as [amlodipine](amlodipine.md). Verapamil is the other rate-limiting CCB (similar cautions, stronger negative inotrope). **Moderate CYP3A4 inhibitor.**

## Dosing (adult, FDA)
| Product / indication | Dose |
|----------------------|------|
| Immediate-release tablets, angina | Start 30 mg four times daily; increase every 1–2 days; usual 180–360 mg/day in 3–4 divided doses |
| Once-daily capsules (Cardizem CD), hypertension | Start 180–240 mg once daily; usual 240–360 mg once daily; some patients respond up to 480 mg once daily |
| Once-daily capsules (Cardizem CD), angina | Start 120 mg or 180 mg once daily; titrate per label |
| Older adults | May need lower doses and slower titration |

- **Modified-release products differ** (twice-daily vs once-daily, different release profiles). UK practice: prescribe MR diltiazem above 60 mg by brand and do not switch brands without review. See [look-alike, sound-alike](../look-alike-sound-alike.md).
- IV diltiazem for atrial fibrillation rate control is a monitored setting — use the label.
- Hepatic and renal impairment: use with caution (extensively hepatically metabolised).

## Contraindications
- Sick sinus syndrome, or second- or third-degree AV block, unless a functioning ventricular pacemaker is present.
- Hypotension (systolic below 90 mmHg).
- Acute MI with pulmonary congestion on chest X-ray.
- Hypersensitivity.
- UK SmPCs also list left ventricular failure with pulmonary congestion, severe bradycardia, combination with IV beta-blockers or dantrolene infusion, ivabradine, and pregnancy/women of child-bearing potential (check the SmPC).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Bradycardia and AV block,** especially with [beta-blockers](bisoprolol.md), [digoxin](digoxin.md) or [amiodarone](amiodarone.md).
- **Heart failure:** negative inotrope — avoid in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction.
- **Hypotension; hepatic injury** (rare); **dermatological reactions** (SJS/TEN reported) — stop if persistent rash.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Beta-blockers, digoxin, amiodarone, ivabradine | Additive bradycardia/AV block — avoid or monitor closely; IV beta-blocker plus diltiazem is dangerous |
| Simvastatin | Higher statin levels, myopathy/rhabdomyolysis. **FDA Zocor label: do not exceed 10 mg simvastatin daily with diltiazem; MHRA (UK): maximum 20 mg/day.** Lovastatin and atorvastatin also affected — use the lowest dose or a non-CYP3A4 statin ([atorvastatin](atorvastatin.md)) |
| CYP3A4 substrates with narrow margin (ciclosporin, tacrolimus, carbamazepine, colchicine, some DOACs, midazolam) | Raised levels — monitor or reduce dose |
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors ([clarithromycin](clarithromycin.md), azole antifungals, ritonavir) | Raised diltiazem levels — bradycardia, hypotension |
| CYP3A4 inducers (rifampicin, [phenytoin](phenytoin.md), [carbamazepine](carbamazepine.md)) | Lower diltiazem levels |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: ankle oedema, headache, dizziness, flushing, bradycardia, constipation (more with verapamil).
- Serious: AV block, severe bradycardia, heart failure, hypotension, SJS/TEN, hepatitis.

## Pregnancy / lactation
UK SmPCs contraindicate use in pregnancy; specialist advice. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Heart rate, blood pressure, ECG (PR interval) when combined with other rate-limiting drugs, signs of heart failure, interacting drugs (especially statins).

## Overdose / antidote
Calcium-channel blocker overdose is life-threatening: bradycardia, AV block, hypotension, hyperglycaemia; MR products cause delayed toxicity. Specialist/poison centre advice — IV calcium, high-dose insulin euglycaemia therapy, vasopressors ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Stay on the same brand. Do not chew or crush modified-release capsules or tablets. Avoid grapefruit juice if your product label says so. Tell any prescriber you take diltiazem — many medicines interact, including some statins and antibiotics. Seek help for fainting, very slow pulse, breathlessness, swelling of legs, or a widespread rash.
