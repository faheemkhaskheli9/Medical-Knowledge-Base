# Isosorbide mononitrate (oral nitrates)

**Class:** Organic nitrate vasodilator, used for angina. Indications and acute-attack use: see label.

## Dosing (adult, FDA)
| Product | Dose |
|---------|------|
| Immediate-release tablets | 20 mg twice daily, with the doses **7 hours apart** (asymmetric dosing gives a nitrate-free interval). A 5 mg starting dose may suit people of particularly small stature, increased to at least 10 mg by day 2–3. Doses above 20 mg twice daily have not been adequately studied |
| Extended-release tablets | Start 30 mg or 60 mg once daily in the morning on rising; 240 mg is rarely needed |
| Renal impairment, hepatic impairment, older adults | See label |

- **Tolerance:** antianginal effect is restored only after nitrates have been absent from the body for several hours — keep to the prescribed schedule.
- Immediate-release and extended-release products are not interchangeable — check the label.

## Contraindications
- **PDE5 inhibitors** (sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil): severe hypotension, syncope or myocardial ischaemia — do not use together. See [sildenafil](sildenafil.md).
- **Riociguat** (soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator): hypotension — do not use together.
- Hypersensitivity or idiosyncratic reactions to other nitrates or nitrites (extended-release label).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- No boxed warning.
- **Acute myocardial infarction or heart failure:** benefit not established; effects are hard to reverse quickly, so not recommended in these settings.
- **Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy:** nitrates may aggravate the angina it causes.
- **Orthostatic hypotension / lightheadedness on standing,** especially just after rising; may be worse with alcohol.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil (PDE5 inhibitors) | Severe hypotension, syncope, myocardial ischaemia — contraindicated |
| Riociguat | Hypotension — contraindicated |
| Alcohol | More frequent lightheadedness on standing |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: headache (most frequent; usually lessens after the first few days), lightheadedness on standing.
- Serious: severe hypotension (especially with PDE5 inhibitors or riociguat), syncope.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Angina frequency, blood pressure (including standing), headache, and any new erectile-dysfunction or pulmonary-hypertension medicines.

## Overdose / antidote
See label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take doses at the times prescribed (for twice-daily tablets, 7 hours apart). **Never take sildenafil, tadalafil or vardenafil (including "erectile dysfunction" tablets bought online) while using this medicine.** Stand up slowly; avoid alcohol. Headache is common at first. Call emergency services for chest pain that does not settle with your usual quick-acting treatment.
