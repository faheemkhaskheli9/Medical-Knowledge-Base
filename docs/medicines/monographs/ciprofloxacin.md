# Ciprofloxacin (fluoroquinolones)

**Class / ATC:** Fluoroquinolone — J01MA02. Others: levofloxacin, moxifloxacin, ofloxacin. WHO AWaRe **Watch** group.

## Dosing (adult, oral)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Complicated UTI / pyelonephritis | 500 mg every 12 h |
| Other infections | 250–750 mg every 12 h depending on site and severity |

Reduce dose in renal impairment (CrCl < 50) — check label.

## Reserve for when alternatives are unsuitable
FDA and MHRA restrict use for uncomplicated cystitis, acute sinusitis, and acute bronchitis because of serious, sometimes permanent, adverse effects.

## Serious adverse effects (boxed warning)
- **Tendinitis/tendon rupture** — higher risk age > 60, corticosteroids, kidney/heart/lung transplant
- **Peripheral neuropathy** (may be permanent)
- **CNS effects**: seizures, psychiatric symptoms
- Exacerbation of **myasthenia gravis** — avoid
- Aortic aneurysm/dissection, QT prolongation, dysglycemia (hypo- and hyperglycemia), *C. difficile*

Stop at first sign of tendon pain, neuropathy, or psychiatric effects.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Antacids, calcium, iron, zinc, magnesium, dairy-only drinks | Reduced absorption — take cipro 2 h before or 6 h after |
| Tizanidine | Contraindicated |
| Theophylline, caffeine, clozapine | Increased levels (CYP1A2 inhibition) |
| Warfarin | Increased INR |
| QT-prolonging drugs | Additive risk |

## Special populations
Children: avoid except specific indications. Pregnancy: generally avoid when alternatives exist.
