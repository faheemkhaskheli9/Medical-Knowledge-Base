# Pioglitazone

**Class:** Thiazolidinedione (glitazone) for type 2 diabetes. See [type 2 diabetes](../../conditions/type-2-diabetes.md).

## Dosing (adult, FDA Actos label)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult without heart failure | Start 15 mg or 30 mg once daily (older label wording; confirm in the current label); titrate in 15 mg steps based on HbA1c |
| Maximum | 45 mg once daily |
| NYHA Class I or II heart failure | Start 15 mg once daily |
| With gemfibrozil or other strong CYP2C8 inhibitors | Maximum 15 mg daily |
| Renal impairment, hepatic impairment, older adults, paediatric | See label |

Tablets: 15 mg, 30 mg, 45 mg.

## Contraindications
- **Initiation in established NYHA Class III or IV heart failure.**
- Other contraindications: see label.
- Do not use in patients with **active bladder cancer** (label warning).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — congestive heart failure:** thiazolidinediones, including pioglitazone, cause or worsen heart failure in some patients. Not recommended in symptomatic heart failure. After starting or increasing the dose, monitor for fluid retention (weight gain, oedema) and heart-failure signs; if heart failure develops, manage it and consider stopping or reducing pioglitazone.
- **Urinary bladder tumours:** possible increased risk; do not use in active bladder cancer, and weigh benefit against unknown recurrence risk in patients with a history of bladder cancer.
- Hepatic effects, fractures and macular oedema have their own label sections — see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Gemfibrozil and other strong CYP2C8 inhibitors | Pioglitazone exposure increased about 3-fold — maximum 15 mg daily |
| Other interactions (including insulin and insulin secretagogues) | See label and [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) |

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label (fluid retention, weight gain and oedema are highlighted in the warnings).
- Serious: new or worsening heart failure, bladder tumours.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
HbA1c; weight, oedema and symptoms of heart failure after starting or increasing the dose; history of bladder cancer (label warning); liver tests and other monitoring per label.

## Overdose / antidote
See label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Report rapid weight gain, swollen ankles or legs, or breathlessness — these can be signs of heart failure. Tell your doctor if you have or have had bladder cancer, or notice blood in your urine. Tell any prescriber you take pioglitazone, particularly if gemfibrozil is suggested.
