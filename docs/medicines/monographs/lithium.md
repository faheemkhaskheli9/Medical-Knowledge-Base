# Lithium

**Class / ATC:** Mood stabilizer — N05AN01. **Narrow therapeutic index.**

## Levels
- Measure **12 hours after the last dose**.
- Usual target 0.6–0.8 mmol/L (up to 1.0 mmol/L in selected patients).
- Check 1 week after starting/dose change, weekly until stable, then every 3 months in the first year, then every 6 months (more often in older adults, interacting drugs, or renal impairment).
- Brands are not interchangeable — prescribe by brand.

## Toxicity (usually > 1.5 mmol/L, can occur within range)
Coarse tremor, vomiting, diarrhea, ataxia, slurred speech, confusion, seizures → **stop lithium, urgent levels, emergency care**.

## Major interactions (raise lithium levels)
NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, thiazide diuretics, dehydration, low-salt diets. See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Monitoring
Renal function (eGFR), thyroid function (TSH), calcium (hyperparathyroidism), weight — before starting and every 6 months.

## Adverse effects
Fine tremor, polyuria/polydipsia (nephrogenic diabetes insipidus), hypothyroidism, weight gain, CKD with long-term use.

## Pregnancy
Small increased risk of cardiac malformation (Ebstein's anomaly); levels change across pregnancy and postpartum — specialist management.

## Patient advice
Maintain hydration and consistent salt intake; seek help with vomiting/diarrhea; avoid OTC NSAIDs; carry a lithium record.
