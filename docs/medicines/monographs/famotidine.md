# Famotidine (H2-receptor antagonists)

**Class / ATC:** Histamine H2-receptor antagonist — A02BA03. Others: nizatidine, cimetidine (many CYP interactions). Ranitidine was withdrawn in the US (FDA, 2020) and many other countries because of NDMA impurity. Less acid suppression than a PPI ([omeprazole](omeprazole.md)).

## Dosing (oral, adult)
| Indication | Dose (FDA) |
|------------|------------|
| Active duodenal ulcer | 40 mg once daily for up to 8 weeks |
| Active gastric ulcer | 40 mg once daily for up to 8 weeks |
| Symptomatic non-erosive GERD | 20 mg twice daily for up to 6 weeks |
| Erosive esophagitis | 20 mg or 40 mg twice daily for up to 12 weeks |
| OTC heartburn (Drug Facts) | 10–20 mg, up to twice in 24 hours; not for more than 14 days without medical advice |

**Renal impairment (FDA, maximum doses):**

| CrCl | Ulcers, erosive esophagitis | Non-erosive GERD |
|------|-----------------------------|------------------|
| 30–60 mL/min | 20 mg once daily or 40 mg every other day | 20 mg once daily |
| < 30 mL/min | 20 mg every other day | 20 mg every other day (alternative 10 mg once daily) |

UK doses differ in places — check the BNF. Children: weight-based — use the label or BNFc.

## Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to famotidine or other H2-receptor antagonists.

## Warnings
- **CNS adverse reactions** (confusion, delirium, hallucinations, seizures) — mainly in renal impairment and older adults; reduce the dose.
- QT prolongation reported in patients with renal impairment.
- Symptom relief **does not exclude gastric cancer**. Investigate alarm features: weight loss, difficulty swallowing, vomiting blood, black stools, anemia, new symptoms at older age ([red flags](../../guidelines/red-flags.md)).

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Drugs that need gastric acid for absorption (e.g. ketoconazole, itraconazole, atazanavir, dasatinib, cefditoren) | Reduced absorption — avoid or separate per label |
| Tizanidine | Famotidine weakly inhibits CYP1A2 — hypotension, bradycardia; avoid (FDA) |

Unlike cimetidine, famotidine has few CYP-mediated interactions.

## Adverse effects
- Common: headache, dizziness, constipation, diarrhea.
- Serious (rare): CNS effects (above), arrhythmia, hypersensitivity, liver enzyme rises.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Generally considered acceptable when an acid suppressant is needed in pregnancy. Low levels in breast milk; acceptable in breastfeeding (LactMed).

## Overdose
Limited data; adverse effects similar to those at normal doses. Supportive care; hemodialysis removes famotidine. See [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling
Can be taken with or without food; for prevention of heartburn take 15–60 minutes before the trigger meal (OTC). Do not use OTC products for more than 14 days without advice. See a doctor promptly for weight loss, difficulty swallowing, vomiting blood or black stools. Tell the prescriber about kidney disease.
