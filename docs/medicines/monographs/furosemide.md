# Furosemide (loop diuretics)

**Class / ATC:** Loop diuretic — C03CA01. Others: bumetanide (1 mg ≈ 40 mg furosemide oral), torasemide.

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Edema (heart failure, cirrhosis, renal) | 20–40 mg PO once daily, adjust to response (lowest dose that keeps the patient euvolemic) |
| Acute pulmonary edema | 20–40 mg IV slowly; higher in patients already on loop diuretics |
| Hypertension | Not first-line; thiazide-like diuretics are preferred |

Give IV slowly (no faster than 4 mg/min at high doses) to limit ototoxicity. Older adults: start low.

## Contraindications
Anuria; severe hypovolemia or dehydration; severe hypokalemia or hyponatremia; hepatic coma.

## Adverse effects
- Common: dehydration, postural hypotension, **hypokalemia**, hyponatremia, hypomagnesemia, raised urate (gout), hyperglycemia
- Serious: acute kidney injury, **ototoxicity** (tinnitus, deafness — high doses, rapid IV, renal impairment), severe electrolyte disturbance

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Digoxin | Hypokalemia increases digoxin toxicity |
| Lithium | Raised lithium levels — avoid or monitor closely |
| Aminoglycosides | Additive ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity |
| NSAIDs | Reduced diuretic effect; AKI with ACE inhibitor/ARB ("triple whammy") |
| QT-prolonging drugs | Risk rises with hypokalemia/hypomagnesemia |

## Monitoring
Weight, fluid status, blood pressure; sodium, potassium, creatinine before starting, within 1–2 weeks, after dose changes, then periodically.

## Patient counselling
Take in the morning (second dose early afternoon) to avoid night-time urination. Weigh daily in heart failure; report rapid gain or loss. Hold and seek advice during vomiting, diarrhea or fever (sick-day rule). Sulfonamide antibiotic allergy is not a reliable reason to avoid it (see [allergy cross-reactivity](../../allergy/cross-reactivity.md)).
