# Zolpidem and zopiclone (Z-drugs)

**Class / ATC:** Non-benzodiazepine hypnotics acting at the GABA-A benzodiazepine site — zolpidem N05CF02, zopiclone N05CF01. Short-term treatment of insomnia only. Compare [diazepam (benzodiazepines)](diazepam.md).

## Dosing (adult)
| Product / population | Dose |
|----------------------|------|
| Zolpidem immediate-release (FDA), women | 5 mg once nightly immediately before bed |
| Zolpidem immediate-release (FDA), men | 5 mg or 10 mg once nightly; maximum 10 mg daily |
| Zolpidem extended-release (FDA) | Women 6.25 mg; men 6.25 mg or 12.5 mg once nightly |
| Zolpidem (FDA), older or debilitated adults, mild to moderate hepatic impairment | 5 mg once nightly |
| Zopiclone (UK SmPC), adult | 7.5 mg at bedtime |
| Zopiclone (UK SmPC), older adults, chronic respiratory insufficiency, hepatic or renal impairment | Start 3.75 mg at bedtime (check SmPC) |

- Take only when able to stay in bed for a full night (7–8 hours for zolpidem IR per FDA label) — less time increases next-day impairment.
- Lower zolpidem doses for women reflect slower clearance (FDA 2013).
- UK SmPCs: the shortest possible course, generally **no longer than 4 weeks including tapering**.
- See [older adults](../../special-populations/older-adults.md) — falls, fractures and confusion.

## Contraindications
- Previous complex sleep behavior (sleep-walking, sleep-driving) on a Z-drug (FDA).
- Hypersensitivity.
- UK SmPCs also list severe hepatic impairment, sleep apnoea syndrome, myasthenia gravis and severe respiratory insufficiency (check the individual SmPC).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Complex sleep behaviors** (boxed warning): sleep-walking, sleep-driving and other activities while not fully awake, including serious injuries and deaths, even at the lowest dose and after one dose. Stop immediately if this happens.
- **Next-day impairment:** driving and alertness may be impaired the next morning, especially with higher doses, ER products, women and older adults.
- **CNS depression:** additive with alcohol, [opioids](opioids.md), benzodiazepines, [gabapentinoids](gabapentinoids.md) — respiratory depression risk.
- **Dependence, tolerance, withdrawal and rebound insomnia;** taper after prolonged use.
- **Depression/suicidality** may worsen; abnormal thinking and behaviour changes.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Alcohol, opioids, other sedatives | Additive sedation, respiratory depression — avoid alcohol; avoid or minimise combinations |
| Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g. ketoconazole, [clarithromycin](clarithromycin.md), ritonavir) | Higher zolpidem/zopiclone levels — consider a lower dose |
| CYP3A4 inducers (rifampicin, [carbamazepine](carbamazepine.md), [phenytoin](phenytoin.md), St John's wort) | Lower levels, reduced effect — rifampicin not recommended with zolpidem |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) and [herb-drug interactions](../herb-drug-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: drowsiness, dizziness, headache, bitter/metallic taste (zopiclone), diarrhoea.
- Serious: complex sleep behaviors, falls and fractures, amnesia, hallucinations, respiratory depression, anaphylaxis/angioedema.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Avoid regular use, especially late in pregnancy (neonatal sedation and withdrawal). Specialist advice. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Ongoing need (review regularly; aim for short courses), next-day sedation, falls, misuse, co-prescribed CNS depressants.

## Overdose / antidote
Sedation to coma, especially with alcohol or other depressants. Supportive care, airway protection; flumazenil can reverse Z-drug effects but carries seizure risk in mixed overdose or dependence — specialist/poison centre advice ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling points
Take just before bed, only if you can sleep a full night. Do not drink alcohol. Do not drive or operate machinery if you still feel drowsy the next day. Stop and tell your doctor if you or others notice you sleep-walk, drive, eat or do other things without remembering. Use for the shortest time; do not stop suddenly after long use without advice. Keep out of reach of others.
