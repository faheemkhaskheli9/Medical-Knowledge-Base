# Meloxicam

**Class:** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). See also [naproxen](naproxen.md) and [ibuprofen](ibuprofen.md) for shared NSAID points.

## Dosing (adult, FDA meloxicam tablets)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis | Start 7.5 mg PO once daily; some patients may benefit from 15 mg once daily |
| Maximum (adult) | **15 mg per day, regardless of formulation** |
| Haemodialysis | Maximum 7.5 mg per day |
| Severe renal impairment (not on dialysis) | Not recommended (not studied) |
| Mild to moderate hepatic impairment | No dose adjustment; severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) not adequately studied |
| Juvenile rheumatoid arthritis | Label versions differ (weight-based 0.125 mg/kg up to 7.5 mg in older/generic labels; 2024 Mobic tablet label: 7.5 mg once daily only for children 60 kg or more) — see label |
| All patients | Lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with treatment goals |

- Meloxicam formulations (tablets, capsules, suspension, injection) are not all interchangeable mg-for-mg — check the product label.

## Contraindications
- Known hypersensitivity to meloxicam (e.g. anaphylactic reactions, serious skin reactions).
- History of asthma, urticaria or other allergic-type reactions after aspirin or other NSAIDs.
- **In the setting of coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) surgery** (peri-operative pain).

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Boxed warning — cardiovascular:** increased risk of serious thrombotic events (myocardial infarction, stroke), which can be fatal; may occur early in treatment and may increase with duration of use.
- **Boxed warning — gastrointestinal:** bleeding, ulceration and perforation, which can be fatal.
- See [renal and hepatic dosing](../../special-populations/renal-hepatic.md) and [older adults](../../special-populations/older-adults.md).

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| [Lithium](lithium.md) | Raised lithium levels and reduced renal lithium clearance (pre-dose lithium and AUC up 21% with meloxicam 15 mg daily in one study) — monitor for lithium toxicity |
| [Methotrexate](methotrexate.md) | Increased methotrexate toxicity (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, renal dysfunction) — monitor |
| Ciclosporin (cyclosporine) | Increased ciclosporin nephrotoxicity — monitor |
| Pemetrexed | Interrupt meloxicam at least 5 days before, the day of and 2 days after pemetrexed (meloxicam tablet label; check the pemetrexed label) |
| Anticoagulants, antiplatelets, aspirin, SSRIs/SNRIs, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, diuretics | Shared NSAID interactions — see [naproxen](naproxen.md) and label |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: GI bleeding, ulceration and perforation; myocardial infarction and stroke; renal impairment; hypersensitivity and serious skin reactions.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Avoid from about 30 weeks of gestation (premature closure of the fetal ductus arteriosus). If an NSAID is needed between about 20 and 30 weeks, use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration; consider amniotic fluid ultrasound if treatment extends beyond 48 hours and stop if oligohydramnios occurs. See [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Blood pressure, renal function, signs of GI bleeding; lithium levels, methotrexate or ciclosporin toxicity when combined.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Do not take more than 15 mg a day, and do not combine with other NSAIDs (including over-the-counter ibuprofen, naproxen or pain-dose aspirin). Seek urgent care for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, chest pain, weakness on one side or slurred speech. Tell your prescriber if you are pregnant, have kidney or heart disease, or take blood thinners, lithium or methotrexate.
