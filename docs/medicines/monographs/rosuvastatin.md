# Rosuvastatin

**Class:** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (statin). See also [atorvastatin](atorvastatin.md) for general statin points.

## Dosing (FDA Crestor label)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Adult | 5–40 mg once daily; choice depends on indication, LDL-C and individual cardiovascular risk |
| Asian patients | Start 5 mg once daily (higher rosuvastatin plasma concentrations) |
| Severe renal impairment, not on haemodialysis | Start 5 mg once daily; do not exceed 10 mg once daily |
| Paediatric, heterozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia | 8 to under 10 years: 5–10 mg once daily; 10 years and older: 5–20 mg once daily |
| With ciclosporin (cyclosporine) | Do not exceed 5 mg once daily |
| With gemfibrozil | Avoid; if used, do not exceed 10 mg once daily |

- UK SmPCs: start 5 mg in patients of Asian ancestry; the 40 mg dose is contraindicated in Asian patients and in patients with predisposing factors for myopathy/rhabdomyolysis (see below).
- Hepatic impairment, older adults: see label.

## Contraindications
- **FDA:** acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis; hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any excipient (rash, pruritus, urticaria and angioedema reported).
- **UK SmPCs (all doses):** active myopathy; severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min). Check the specific product SmPC for the full list.
- **UK SmPCs (40 mg dose):** patients of Asian ancestry; predisposing factors for myopathy/rhabdomyolysis, including moderate renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 60 mL/min), hypothyroidism, personal or family history of hereditary muscle disorders, previous muscle toxicity with another statin or a fibrate, alcohol abuse, and situations where plasma levels may rise.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- No boxed warning.
- **Myopathy and rhabdomyolysis:** risk increased with ciclosporin or gemfibrozil (see dose limits above).
- **Immune-mediated necrotising myopathy** (rare): persistent weakness with raised creatine kinase; stop rosuvastatin if suspected — immunosuppressive treatment may be needed.
- Hepatic dysfunction and proteinuria/haematuria have their own label sections — see label.

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| Ciclosporin | Rosuvastatin exposure (AUC) increased about 7-fold — maximum 5 mg once daily (FDA) |
| Gemfibrozil | Increased myopathy risk — avoid; if used, maximum 10 mg once daily (FDA) |
| Aluminium/magnesium hydroxide antacids | Give rosuvastatin at least 2 hours before the antacid (FDA) |
| Other interacting drugs | Some UK SmPCs also list ticagrelor and others; check the product SmPC and [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md) |

## Adverse effects
- Common: see label.
- Serious: myopathy, rhabdomyolysis, immune-mediated necrotising myopathy, hypersensitivity reactions (including angioedema).

## Pregnancy / lactation
- Lactation: breastfeeding is not recommended during treatment (current FDA label).
- Pregnancy: labeling has changed across versions — check the current label and see [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Lipid response; muscle symptoms (check creatine kinase if symptomatic); renal function before choosing the dose; interacting drugs (ciclosporin, gemfibrozil). Liver tests: see label.

## Overdose / antidote
No specific antidote; supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take once daily as prescribed. Report unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness promptly. Tell any prescriber you take rosuvastatin — some medicines (for example ciclosporin and gemfibrozil) limit the dose. If you take an aluminium/magnesium antacid, take rosuvastatin at least 2 hours before it.
