# Losartan (angiotensin II receptor blockers)

**Class / ATC:** ARB — C09CA01. Others: valsartan, candesartan, irbesartan, telmisartan, olmesartan.

## Dosing (adult, oral, once daily)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Hypertension | 50 mg; 25 mg if volume-depleted or hepatic impairment; max 100 mg |
| CKD with albuminuria, diabetic nephropathy | Titrate to highest tolerated dose (up to 100 mg) |
| Heart failure (ACE inhibitor intolerance) | Start low, titrate to target (candesartan/valsartan have stronger HF evidence) |

Used when an ACE inhibitor causes cough. See [ACE inhibitors](ace-inhibitors.md).

## Contraindications
**Pregnancy** (boxed warning — fetal renal toxicity and death; stop as soon as pregnancy is detected); combination with aliskiren in diabetes or eGFR < 60; previous angioedema on an ARB.

## Warnings
- Do not routinely combine with an ACE inhibitor (more hyperkalemia and AKI, no extra benefit).
- Bilateral renal artery stenosis: risk of AKI.
- Angioedema is rarer than with ACE inhibitors but can occur, including in patients who had it on an ACE inhibitor.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Potassium supplements, spironolactone, eplerenone, trimethoprim, potassium-containing salt substitutes | Hyperkalemia |
| NSAIDs + diuretic | AKI ("triple whammy"); reduced BP effect |
| Lithium | Raised lithium levels |

## Monitoring
Potassium and creatinine/eGFR before starting, 1–2 weeks after starting or each dose increase, then at least annually. A creatinine rise up to 30% is acceptable; more than that — review (NICE/KDIGO).

## Patient counselling
Hold during vomiting, diarrhea or dehydration (sick-day rule) and restart when eating and drinking normally. Use contraception if pregnancy is possible; stop and seek advice if pregnant.
