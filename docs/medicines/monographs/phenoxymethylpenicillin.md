# Phenoxymethylpenicillin (penicillin V)

**Class:** Narrow-spectrum oral penicillin (beta-lactam), WHO AWaRe **Access** antibiotic — see [antimicrobial stewardship](../../infections/antimicrobial-stewardship.md). Mainly active against streptococci; not reliable for serious infections because oral absorption varies. US name: penicillin V potassium.

## Dosing (adult)
| Population | Dose |
|------------|------|
| **Acute sore throat when an antibiotic is indicated (NICE NG84, adults 18 and over)** | **500 mg PO four times daily, or 1000 mg PO twice daily, for 5 to 10 days** (5 days may be enough for symptoms; 10 days may improve microbiological cure) |
| Other indications (e.g. dental, skin, rheumatic fever prophylaxis) | See BNF / local guideline |
| US labels | Regimens and units differ (some products are labelled in units as well as mg) — see label |
| Paediatric | Age-banded doses — see BNF for Children / label |
| Renal impairment, pregnancy, breastfeeding | See BNF (NICE refers to the BNF for these populations) |

- Take on an empty stomach (food reduces absorption) — see label for timing.
- Penicillin allergy: NICE NG84 alternative for adults is clarithromycin (see [clarithromycin](clarithromycin.md)); check [cross-reactivity](../../allergy/cross-reactivity.md).

## Contraindications
- **Hypersensitivity to any penicillin.** Check history carefully; see [cross-reactivity](../../allergy/cross-reactivity.md) for cephalosporin and other beta-lactam cross-reactivity.

## Warnings / boxed warnings
- **Anaphylaxis and serious hypersensitivity** reactions (more likely with a history of penicillin, cephalosporin or multiple allergen sensitivity).
- ***Clostridioides difficile*-associated diarrhoea** can occur during or after treatment.
- Not suitable for severe, deep-seated infections or when gastrointestinal absorption is unreliable (vomiting, gastric dilatation).

## Major interactions
| Drug | Effect / action |
|------|-----------------|
| [Methotrexate](methotrexate.md) | Penicillins reduce methotrexate excretion — risk of methotrexate toxicity; monitor |
| [Warfarin](warfarin.md) | INR may change during antibiotic courses — monitor |
| Oral typhoid vaccine (live) | Antibiotics may inactivate it — see vaccine guidance |
| Probenecid | Raises penicillin levels (sometimes used intentionally) |

See [major interactions](../../interactions/major-interactions.md).

## Adverse effects
- Common: nausea, diarrhoea, rash — see label.
- Serious: anaphylaxis, angioedema, serious skin reactions, *C. difficile* infection — see label.

## Pregnancy / lactation
See BNF/label and [pregnancy and lactation](../../special-populations/pregnancy-lactation.md).

## Monitoring
Clinical response (reassess if not improving or worsening); allergy symptoms; diarrhoea. Red flags in sore throat (stridor, drooling, difficulty swallowing saliva, trismus — see [red flags](../../guidelines/red-flags.md)) need urgent assessment.

## Overdose / antidote
Mainly gastrointestinal upset; large overdoses of penicillins can cause neurotoxicity, especially in renal impairment — supportive care — see label and [antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md).

## Patient counselling points
Take on an empty stomach, spaced evenly through the day, and finish the course you were prescribed. Tell your prescriber about any previous reaction to penicillin. Stop and seek urgent help for swelling of the face, lips or throat, difficulty breathing or a widespread rash. Contact your doctor for severe or bloody diarrhoea, or if you cannot swallow fluids or your saliva.
