# Omeprazole (proton pump inhibitors)

**Class / ATC:** Proton pump inhibitor — A02BC01. Others: esomeprazole, lansoprazole, pantoprazole, rabeprazole.

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| GERD / esophagitis | 20 mg once daily for 4–8 weeks (40 mg for severe esophagitis) |
| Peptic ulcer | 20–40 mg once daily for 4–8 weeks |
| *H. pylori* eradication | Twice daily as part of combination therapy per local guideline (bismuth quadruple therapy commonly first-line) |
| NSAID ulcer prophylaxis | 20 mg once daily |

Take 30–60 minutes **before breakfast**. Review need regularly and step down/deprescribe when appropriate.

## Red flags needing endoscopy/referral
Difficulty or painful swallowing, GI bleeding (black stools, vomiting blood), unintentional weight loss, persistent vomiting, iron-deficiency anemia, new symptoms at age ≥ 55–60.

## Long-term risks
*C. difficile* infection, hypomagnesemia, vitamin B12 deficiency, fractures; rebound acid hypersecretion on abrupt stopping.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Clopidogrel | Reduced activation (omeprazole/esomeprazole) — prefer pantoprazole |
| Methotrexate (high dose) | Increased levels |
| Rilpivirine | Contraindicated |
| Atazanavir, ketoconazole, itraconazole, some kinase inhibitors | Reduced absorption (need acid) |
