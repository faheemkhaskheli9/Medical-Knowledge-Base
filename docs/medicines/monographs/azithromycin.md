# Azithromycin

**Class / ATC:** Macrolide — J01FA10. WHO AWaRe **Watch** group.

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Respiratory infection / CAP (with other agents per guideline) | 500 mg day 1, then 250 mg days 2–5; or 500 mg daily for 3 days |
| Chlamydia (alternative; doxycycline is preferred per CDC 2021) | 1 g single dose |
| Typhoid (incl. XDR, uncomplicated) | Per national guideline — see [Pakistan](../../regional/pakistan.md) |

Long tissue half-life — short courses give prolonged effect.

## Warnings
- **QT prolongation and torsades** — caution with QT-prolonging drugs, hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia, bradycardia, known long QT.
- *C. difficile*, hepatotoxicity (rare).
- Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis in neonates.
- Resistance: widespread pneumococcal and *Mycoplasma* macrolide resistance in some regions.

## Interactions
Fewer CYP3A4 interactions than clarithromycin/erythromycin. Additive QT risk with antiarrhythmics, antipsychotics, ondansetron, fluoroquinolones, methadone.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Generally considered compatible.
