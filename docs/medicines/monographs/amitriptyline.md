# Amitriptyline (tricyclic antidepressants)

**Class / ATC:** Tricyclic antidepressant (TCA) — N06AA09. Others: nortriptyline, imipramine, clomipramine, dosulepin. Strongly anticholinergic and sedating. **Very toxic in overdose.**

## Dosing (oral, adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Depression (FDA) | 75 mg per day in divided doses, increased if needed to 150 mg per day; or 50–100 mg at bedtime. Hospitalised patients up to 300 mg per day. Adolescents and older adults: 10 mg three times daily with 20 mg at bedtime may suffice |
| Neuropathic pain (BNF, NICE CG173) | 10 mg once daily at night, increased gradually; usual 25–75 mg at night. Higher doses under specialist supervision |
| Migraine prophylaxis, tension-type headache (BNF, off-label in many countries) | Start 10 mg at night and titrate slowly |

- Low pain doses still carry interaction, anticholinergic and overdose risks.
- Older adults: start low (10 mg at night); avoid where possible (Beers — strongly anticholinergic, sedating, orthostatic hypotension) ([older adults](../../special-populations/older-adults.md)).
- Hepatic impairment: use with caution; avoid in severe impairment (BNF).

## Contraindications
- Use of an **MAOI** within the past 14 days (serotonin syndrome); linezolid or IV methylene blue.
- **Recovery phase after myocardial infarction**; heart block, arrhythmias.
- Hypersensitivity to amitriptyline. BNF also lists mania and severe liver disease.

## Warnings
- **Boxed warning — suicidality** in children, adolescents and young adults (under 25) with antidepressants; monitor closely at start and dose changes. Not approved for depression under 12 years (FDA). See [crisis support](../../mental-health/crisis-support.md).
- Prescribe limited quantities to people at risk of self-harm — overdose is often fatal.
- QT prolongation and arrhythmia; caution with cardiovascular disease.
- Anticholinergic effects: urinary retention, constipation, angle-closure glaucoma, confusion.
- Lowers seizure threshold; may trigger mania in bipolar disorder.
- Hyponatremia (especially older adults); serotonin syndrome with other serotonergic drugs.
- Withdrawal symptoms on abrupt stopping — taper.

## Interactions
| Drug | Effect |
|------|--------|
| MAOIs, linezolid | Contraindicated — serotonin syndrome, hypertensive crisis |
| Fluoxetine, paroxetine, bupropion, quinidine (CYP2D6 inhibitors) | Raise TCA levels — toxicity |
| [Sertraline](sertraline.md), tramadol, triptans ([sumatriptan](sumatriptan.md)) | Serotonin syndrome risk |
| QT-prolonging drugs (e.g. [ondansetron](ondansetron.md), macrolides, antipsychotics) | Additive QT risk |
| Alcohol, opioids, [benzodiazepines](diazepam.md), [gabapentinoids](gabapentinoids.md) | Additive sedation, respiratory depression |
| Other anticholinergics | Additive anticholinergic burden |

## Pharmacogenomics
CYP2D6 poor or ultrarapid metabolisers and CYP2C19 variants alter levels — CPIC suggests avoiding or adjusting doses ([gene–drug pairs](../../pharmacogenomics/gene-drug-pairs.md)).

## Adverse effects
- Common: drowsiness, dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision, weight gain, dizziness on standing.
- Serious: arrhythmia, seizures, urinary retention, delirium, hyponatremia, suicidality.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Specialist decision; neonatal withdrawal or anticholinergic effects possible with use near term. Low levels in breast milk; usually considered acceptable — monitor the infant for sedation.

## Monitoring
Mood and suicidality early on; ECG if cardiac disease or higher doses; sodium in older adults.

## Overdose / antidote
Life-threatening: QRS widening, ventricular arrhythmia, seizures, coma, hypotension, anticholinergic syndrome. **Sodium bicarbonate** for QRS widening or arrhythmia. Contact a poison center ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Patient counselling
Take at night (drowsiness). For pain it may take 2–6 weeks for benefit; for depression several weeks. Do not stop suddenly. Avoid alcohol; do not drive if drowsy. Keep tablets safely stored — overdose is dangerous. Seek help urgently for thoughts of self-harm, fainting, palpitations or inability to pass urine.
