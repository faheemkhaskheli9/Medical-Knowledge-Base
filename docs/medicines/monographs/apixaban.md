# Apixaban

**Class / ATC:** Direct oral anticoagulant, factor Xa inhibitor — B01AF02 — **high-alert medication**

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Dose |
|------------|------|
| Non-valvular atrial fibrillation | 5 mg PO twice daily |
| AF dose reduction | 2.5 mg twice daily if **≥ 2 of**: age ≥ 80 years, weight ≤ 60 kg, serum creatinine ≥ 1.5 mg/dL (133 µmol/L) |
| VTE treatment | 10 mg twice daily for 7 days, then 5 mg twice daily |
| Extended VTE prevention (after ≥ 6 months treatment) | 2.5 mg twice daily |
| VTE prophylaxis after hip/knee replacement | 2.5 mg twice daily |

Missed dose: take as soon as remembered on the same day, then resume twice daily; do not double.

## Do not use
- Mechanical heart valves; moderate-severe mitral stenosis (use warfarin).
- Triple-positive antiphospholipid syndrome.
- Active major bleeding.

## Interactions
| Drug | Action |
|------|--------|
| Strong CYP3A4 + P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir) | Reduce dose by 50%; avoid if already on 2.5 mg twice daily |
| Strong CYP3A4 + P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin, St John's wort) | Avoid |
| Antiplatelets, NSAIDs, SSRIs | Increased bleeding |

## Reversal
Andexanet alfa or 4-factor PCC for life-threatening bleeding ([antidotes](../../toxicology/antidotes.md)).

## Peri-procedural
Hold 24–48 h before procedures depending on bleeding risk and renal function; no bridging with heparin usually needed.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Avoid — use LMWH.
