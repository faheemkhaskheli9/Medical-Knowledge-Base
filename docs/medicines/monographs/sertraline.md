# Sertraline

**Class / ATC:** Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) — N06AB06

## Dosing (adult)
| Indication | Start | Range |
|------------|-------|-------|
| Depression, OCD | 50 mg once daily | 50–200 mg/day |
| Panic disorder, PTSD, social anxiety | 25 mg once daily for 1 week, then 50 mg | 50–200 mg/day |

Increase no faster than weekly. Benefit usually starts in 2–4 weeks; full effect may take 6–8 weeks. Continue ≥ 6 months after remission for depression.

## Boxed warning
Increased suicidal thoughts and behavior in children, adolescents, and young adults (< 25 years) — monitor closely, especially early and after dose changes.

## Contraindications / interactions
- **MAO inhibitors** (including linezolid, IV methylene blue): contraindicated — 14-day gap.
- Pimozide: contraindicated.
- Serotonergic drugs (tramadol, triptans, St John's wort): serotonin syndrome risk.
- NSAIDs, aspirin, anticoagulants: increased bleeding — consider gastroprotection.

## Adverse effects
Nausea, diarrhea, insomnia, sexual dysfunction, sweating; **hyponatremia** (older adults); activation/agitation early in treatment; switch to mania in bipolar disorder.

## Stopping
Taper gradually over weeks to avoid discontinuation symptoms (dizziness, irritability, "electric shock" sensations, flu-like symptoms).

## Pregnancy / lactation
Widely used in pregnancy when benefits outweigh risks (small risks: neonatal adaptation syndrome, persistent pulmonary hypertension). Among the preferred SSRIs in breastfeeding (low milk levels).
