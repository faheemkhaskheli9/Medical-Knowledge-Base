# Vaccination

!!! warning "Schedules are national"
    Always check the **current national schedule** (e.g. EPI Pakistan, UKHSA Green Book, CDC/ACIP, NHS). Schedules change, and US recommendations changed several times in 2025.

## WHO routine childhood antigens (all countries)
BCG (where TB is endemic), polio (OPV/IPV), DTP-containing vaccine, hepatitis B (birth dose + series), Hib, pneumococcal conjugate, rotavirus, measles (2 doses), rubella, HPV (girls 9–14; single-dose schedule accepted by WHO since 2022).

Region- or risk-dependent: typhoid conjugate, Japanese encephalitis, yellow fever, meningococcal, dengue, malaria (RTS,S or R21 in endemic areas).

## Pakistan EPI — childhood schedule (verify with EPI before use)
| Age | Vaccines |
|-----|----------|
| Birth | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B |
| 6 weeks | OPV-1, Pentavalent-1 (DTP-HepB-Hib), PCV-1, Rotavirus-1 |
| 10 weeks | OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, PCV-2, Rotavirus-2 |
| 14 weeks | OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, PCV-3, IPV-1 |
| 9 months | Measles-Rubella (MR)-1, Typhoid conjugate vaccine (TCV), IPV-2 |
| 15 months | MR-2 |

Plus polio supplementary campaigns (door-to-door OPV), and tetanus-diphtheria (Td) for women of childbearing age and in pregnancy.

## Adults (common recommendations; verify locally)
| Vaccine | Typical recommendation |
|---------|------------------------|
| Influenza | Annually — priority for older adults, pregnancy, chronic disease, health workers |
| Tetanus-diphtheria (Td/Tdap) | Booster per national schedule; pertussis-containing vaccine in **every pregnancy** where recommended |
| Pneumococcal | Older adults and people with risk conditions |
| Herpes zoster (recombinant) | Older adults and immunocompromised adults (age thresholds vary) |
| Hepatitis B | Unvaccinated adults at risk; health workers |
| HPV | Catch-up per national program |
| RSV | Older adults and pregnancy (maternal vaccine) in some countries |
| COVID-19 | Per current national advice |

## Contraindications and precautions
- **Contraindication:** anaphylaxis to a previous dose or a vaccine component.
- **Live vaccines** (MMR/MR, varicella, BCG, yellow fever, oral typhoid, live zoster): avoid in pregnancy and significant immunosuppression.
- Two live parenteral vaccines: give the same day or ≥ 4 weeks apart.
- Minor illness without fever is **not** a reason to delay.
- Egg allergy is not a contraindication to influenza vaccine ([allergy](../allergy/cross-reactivity.md)).
- Vaccines do not cause autism — this has been extensively studied and refuted.
