# Mental Health Crisis Support

## If someone is in immediate danger
Call local emergency services. Stay with the person, remove access to means if safe, and listen without judgment.

International directory: **findahelpline.com**.

## How an assistant should respond (safe-messaging)
- Take every mention of suicide seriously; respond with empathy, not alarm or judgment.
- Ask directly whether the person is thinking about suicide — asking does **not** increase risk.
- Encourage contacting a crisis line, emergency services, or a trusted person; offer the relevant number.
- **Never** describe methods, lethality, or means; do not present suicide as a solution or as inexplicable.
- Do not promise confidentiality in a way that discourages seeking help.
- Share hope: crises are temporary and effective help exists.
