# Metformin

**Class / ATC:** Biguanide — A10BA02
**Mechanism:** Decreases hepatic glucose production, improves insulin sensitivity.

## Dosing (adult, immediate release)
- Start 500 mg PO once or twice daily with meals (or 850 mg once daily); increase gradually to reduce GI effects.
- Max: 2550 mg/day IR (US label); 2000 mg/day extended release.

## Renal function (eGFR, mL/min/1.73 m²)
| eGFR | Action |
|------|--------|
| ≥ 45 | Standard use |
| 30–44 | Do not initiate; if already taking, assess benefit/risk, consider dose reduction |
| < 30 | Contraindicated |

## Iodinated contrast
Hold at time of or before the procedure if eGFR 30–60, liver disease, alcoholism, heart failure, or intra-arterial contrast; re-evaluate eGFR 48 h after and restart if renal function is stable.

## Warnings
- Lactic acidosis (rare) — risk with renal impairment, hypoxia, sepsis, excess alcohol, hepatic impairment.
- Vitamin B12 deficiency with long-term use — monitor periodically.
- Hold during acute illness with dehydration ("sick day rules").

## Adverse effects
GI upset (diarrhea, nausea), metallic taste. Does not cause hypoglycemia alone.
