# Paracetamol (acetaminophen)

**Names:** paracetamol (INN, UK/EU/Asia), acetaminophen (US/Canada). Brands: Panadol, Tylenol, Calpol.
**Class / ATC:** Analgesic, antipyretic — N02BE01

## Dosing
| Population | Dose | Max |
|------------|------|-----|
| Adult ≥ 50 kg | 500 mg–1 g PO every 4–6 h | 4 g / 24 h |
| Adult < 50 kg, hepatic risk factors (chronic alcohol use, malnutrition, liver disease) | Reduce dose (e.g. 15 mg/kg per dose) | Lower maximum per local guidance (often ≤ 3 g / 24 h) |
| Children | 10–15 mg/kg per dose every 4–6 h | 60–75 mg/kg/24 h depending on jurisdiction, not above adult max |

Minimum interval 4 hours. IV dosing differs by weight — check product label.

## Key warnings
- **Hepatotoxicity** in overdose; many combination products (cold/flu, opioid combinations) contain paracetamol — count the total.
- Overdose may be asymptomatic for the first 24 h.

## Overdose
- Antidote: **acetylcysteine (N-acetylcysteine)**.
- Single acute ingestion: plot serum level at ≥ 4 h post-ingestion on the treatment nomogram.
- Staggered or unknown-time ingestions: treat per toxicology guidance; contact poison center.

## Pregnancy / lactation
Generally considered the analgesic/antipyretic of choice in pregnancy at the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration. Compatible with breastfeeding.
