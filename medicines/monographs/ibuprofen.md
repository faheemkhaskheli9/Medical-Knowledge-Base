# Ibuprofen

**Class / ATC:** NSAID (non-selective COX inhibitor) — M01AE01

## Dosing
| Population | Dose | Max |
|------------|------|-----|
| Adult, OTC | 200–400 mg PO every 4–6 h with food | 1200 mg / 24 h |
| Adult, prescription | 400–800 mg PO every 6–8 h | 3200 mg / 24 h (US); 2400 mg / 24 h (UK BNF usual max) |
| Children ≥ 6 months | 5–10 mg/kg every 6–8 h | 40 mg/kg / 24 h |

## Contraindications / avoid
- Active GI bleeding or peptic ulcer; history of NSAID-induced asthma/urticaria.
- Severe heart failure, severe renal or hepatic impairment.
- Peri-operative CABG (US boxed warning).
- Pregnancy: avoid from 20 weeks (oligohydramnios, fetal renal dysfunction); contraindicated in third trimester (premature ductus arteriosus closure).

## Boxed warnings (US)
Increased risk of serious cardiovascular thrombotic events and serious GI bleeding/ulceration/perforation.

## Major interactions
- Anticoagulants/antiplatelets/SSRIs → bleeding risk.
- ACE inhibitor/ARB + diuretic + NSAID ("triple whammy") → acute kidney injury.
- Lithium, methotrexate → increased levels/toxicity.
- May reduce antiplatelet effect of low-dose aspirin if taken before it.
